1. 핵심 요약

Woofpedia는 계정·로그인이 없으며, 이용자의 개인정보를 직접 수집·저장하지 않습니다(광고 식별자·앱 사용 통계는 Google AdMob·Firebase가 처리). 즐겨찾기·설정은 기기 안에만 저장되며, 1.1.0 버전부터 기기 백업·기기 간 이전 대상에서도 제외됩니다.

2. 앱이 수집하지 않는 것

  • 이름·이메일·전화번호 등 계정 정보 — 요구하지 않음(로그인 없음).
  • 즐겨찾기·퀴즈 기록 — 서버로 전송하지 않음(기기 내 로컬 저장만).

설정값과 즐겨찾기는 기기 내부에만 저장되며 앱 삭제 시 함께 삭제됩니다.

3. 제3자 서비스 (광고·결제)

  • Google AdMob(광고): 무료 제공을 위해 광고를 표시하며, 광고 제공·측정을 위해 광고 식별자(Advertising ID) 및 기기 정보를 수집·처리할 수 있습니다. EEA/영국 사용자에게는 동의(UMP)를 표시합니다. '광고 제거' 구매 시 배너·전면 광고가 비활성화됩니다(보상형 광고는 사용자가 선택할 때만 재생).
  • Google Firebase Analytics(앱 사용 통계): 앱 개선을 위해 앱 실행·화면 이동 등 사용 통계와 기기 정보(기기 모델·OS 버전·대략적 지역 등), 앱 인스턴스 ID 가 Google 서버로 전송됩니다. 개발자는 개인을 식별할 수 없는 집계 통계만 봅니다. Google 개인정보처리방침
  • Google Play 결제: '광고 제거'·'전체 해금' 인앱 구매는 Google Play가 처리하며, 앱은 결제 정보를 저장하지 않습니다.

특정 연령을 대상으로 하지 않으며 개인정보를 직접 수집하지 않습니다.

English

1. Summary

Woofpedia has no accounts or login and does not itself collect or store personal information (advertising identifiers and app usage statistics are handled by Google AdMob and Firebase). Your favorites and settings are stored only on your device, and from version 1.1.0 they are also excluded from device backup and device-to-device transfer.

2. What the app does NOT collect

  • Account info such as name, email, or phone number — never requested (no login).
  • Your favorites or quiz history — never sent to any server (local, on-device only).

Settings and favorites stay on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Third-party services (Ads & Purchases)

  • Google AdMob (ads): To keep the app free, ads are shown. AdMob may collect and process the Advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads. A consent form (UMP) is shown to EEA/UK users. Purchasing "Remove Ads" disables banner and interstitial ads (rewarded ads play only when the user chooses).
  • Google Firebase Analytics (app usage statistics): To improve the app, usage statistics such as app opens and screen views, device information (device model, OS version, approximate region) and an app instance ID are sent to Google servers. The developer only sees aggregated statistics that do not identify you. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: The "Remove Ads" and "Unlock everything" in-app purchases are processed by Google Play; the app does not store any payment information.

4. Children

This app is not directed to a specific age group and does not collect personal information.

Records created by this app are stored only on the user's device. The developer does not receive them on any server and does not retain them. Data kept on the device remains until the user deletes it in the app or uninstalls the app, at which point it is removed with the app. Identifiers and information used for ads and app usage statistics are handled by Google (AdMob, Firebase) and its advertising partners under their own policies and retention periods; the developer does not retain them.
