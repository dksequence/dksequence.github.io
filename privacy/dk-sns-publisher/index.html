Privacy Policy (English)

What this is

DK SNS Publisher is an internal tool DKsequence uses to publish posts about its own apps to its own accounts (Threads, Instagram, Facebook Page). It is not a service for the public to sign in to.

Data we collect

The tool does not collect or store personal data about visitors or users. It only keeps the access tokens of DKsequence's own connected accounts on the operator's computer, and never shares or sells them.

Data deletion

If you believe the tool holds information about you, email dkseq7@gmail.com and we will delete it and reply. You can also remove the app at any time in your Threads, Instagram or Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.
