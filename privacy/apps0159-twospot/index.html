TwoSpot — Privacy Policy

App: TwoSpot (틀린 그림 찾기) · Package: com.dksequence.twospot
Last updated: 2026-08-10 · Developer: DKSEQUENCE · Contact: kitan98@gmail.com

1. Summary

TwoSpot keeps your progress on your own device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Third-party services used inside the app (advertising and in-app purchases) may process limited data as described below.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (which puzzles you cleared), your language choice, sound and music settings, and whether you bought the ad removal are stored locally using Android's app storage. This data never leaves your device through us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Third-party services

4. Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, precise location, or files. Internet access is used only by the advertising and purchase services above.

5. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads served in the app are configured through Google AdMob and follow Google Play Families policy requirements where applicable.

6. Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Purchasing "Remove ads" stops the banner and the interstitial; the optional rewarded video for hints remains available if you choose it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

데이터 보관 / Data retention

이 앱이 만드는 기록은 사용자의 기기 안에만 저장됩니다. 개발자는 사용자의 기록을 서버로 받지 않으며 따로 보관하지 않습니다. 기기에 저장된 기록은 사용자가 앱에서 삭제하거나 앱을 제거할 때까지 남아 있고, 삭제하면 함께 사라집니다. 광고에 쓰이는 식별자는 Google 및 광고 파트너가 각자의 정책과 보관 기간에 따라 처리하며, 개발자가 보관하지 않습니다.

Records created by this app are stored only on the user's device. The developer does not receive them on any server and does not retain them. Data kept on the device remains until the user deletes it in the app or uninstalls the app, at which point it is removed with the app. Advertising identifiers used for ads are handled by Google and its advertising partners under their own policies and retention periods; the developer does not retain them.
