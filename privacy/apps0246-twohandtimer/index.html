TwoHand Cube Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30 · App: TwoHand Cube Timer (com.dksequence.twohandcubetimer) · Developer: DKsequence

TwoHand Cube Timer works without any account or sign-in, and the developer does not directly collect or store users' personal information.

1. Information we collect

  • The developer does not directly collect personal information. Solve records, sessions, skin, language, ready delay and other settings are stored only on your device.
  • Local storage: records and settings are kept in the app's private storage, are not sent to the developer's servers, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • CSV copy: records are copied to your device clipboard only when you choose to copy them as CSV; nothing is sent to the developer.
  • Permissions: the app does not request sensitive permissions such as camera, location, contacts or storage.
  • Microphone: requested only if you turn on “Thinking vs handling from sound” in Settings. Sound during a solve is analyzed only on this device; the recording is deleted right after analysis and is never saved or sent. Only the ratio numbers are shown.
  • Notifications: used only for a single reminder if you have not opened the app for 7 days (can be turned off in Settings).
  • In-app purchase: the Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play; the developer does not receive your payment details.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The app shows Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded). The timer and duel screens do not display advertisements.
  • AdMob may collect the Advertising ID, device information and app usage information to serve and measure ads (per Google policy).
  • Through AdMob mediation, Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) take part in ad bidding and may process the Advertising ID and device information under their own policies. Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy · Liftoff: https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/
  • Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your ad consent, which you can change later.
  • Google policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · Controls: device Settings > Google > Ads.

3. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

  • To improve the app, Firebase Analytics may collect anonymous usage statistics (app interactions such as launches and screen use, and device identifiers).
  • We do not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address.
  • Details: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Third parties

  • Other than ads (AdMob and its mediation partners Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize), analytics (Firebase) and payments (Google Play) above, no data is shared with third parties.

5. Children

  • This app is intended for general users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. The developer does not directly collect children's personal information.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Data retention: records and settings are not collected or retained by the developer; they stay on this device until you delete them.
  • On-device data such as records and settings is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also delete single solves or clear a session on the Records screen.
  • Advertising and analytics data are handled under the policies of each provider (Google).
