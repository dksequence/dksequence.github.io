How to escape a bad date

Decide within the first 30 minutes whether you want to stay. If not, end it kindly and clearly: thank them, say it is not a match, and leave. If you feel uncomfortable at any point, you can leave right away. A pre-arranged call from a friend gives you a ready exit.

Why this happens

Most bad dates are not dangerous, just a mismatch. Staying for three more hours out of politeness helps nobody, and the other person usually prefers a clear, kind ending to a long, strained evening.

A few things make the exit easier: planning it before you arrive, meeting somewhere public, and having your own way home. The steps below work with a friend and a phone.

How to fix it without an app

  1. Plan the exit before you arrive

    Meet in a public place with plenty of people around, tell a friend or family member where you are going and when you expect to be there, and arrange your own way home. Dating-safety guides give the same advice on telling a friend and choosing a busy place.

    Time: 5 minutes · You need: A friend and your own transport

  2. Set a check-in with your friend

    Agree that your friend will text or call at a set time, for example 45 minutes in. If you want to stay, ignore it. If you want to leave, you have a reason ready that does not require inventing a story on the spot.

    Time: 2 minutes to set up · You need: A friend who is available

  3. Decide early, not late

    Check in with yourself after the first half hour. If you keep watching the clock, you have your answer. The longer you wait, the harder it is to end kindly.

    Time: 30 minutes in · You need: Honesty with yourself

  4. End it kindly and clearly

    Say something simple and true: “I enjoyed meeting you, but I don't think we are a match. I'm going to head out.” Thank them, pay your share, and go. Do not promise a second date you do not want.

    Time: 1 minute · You need: One short sentence

  5. Leave at once if you feel unsafe

    If you feel uncomfortable or sense bad vibes at any point, you do not owe anyone a long explanation. A university student-life guide puts it plainly: “If you feel uncomfortable or have bad vibes at any point during the date, you can always leave!”

    Time: Right away · You need: Your own transport and a phone

Where this approach stops working

  • The friend check-in depends on a friend who is awake, available and reliable. Friends forget, are busy, or are asleep.
  • A scripted line is easy to write and hard to say. Many people go blank when it is time to say the sentence face to face.
  • Some situations are hard to end politely, such as when your date keeps talking or asks you to stay for another drink.
  • A text from a friend is easy to ignore or to read as casual, and a notification buzz does not look like a call you are obliged to take.
  • No exit trick replaces your own judgment. A fake call is a polite exit for an awkward evening, not a safety system, and it should not be used to deceive someone in a harmful way.

Questions people also ask

How do I end a date politely?

Say something short and true: thank them for their time, say you do not think it is a match, and leave. A clear, kind sentence is easier on both of you than hinting for an hour.

Is it okay to leave a date early?

Yes. A university student-life guide says that if you feel uncomfortable or have bad vibes at any point during the date, you can always leave.

How can I plan an exit before the date starts?

Meet in a public place, tell a friend where you will be, and arrange your own way home. Agree on a check-in with your friend at a set time.

Is a fake call a good way to escape a bad date?

It can give you a polite moment to step away, and it is easier than inventing an excuse on the spot. It is better to be honest when you can, and it should not be used to deceive or harm anyone.
