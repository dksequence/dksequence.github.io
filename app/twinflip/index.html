English

Twinflip is a learning game where you flip cards, match pairs, and pick up hiragana and katakana. Your progress (letters finished, streak, memory album, settings) is stored only on your device and is never uploaded or transmitted. There is no sign-in and the app itself does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. As described below, advertising data such as the Advertising ID and app usage events for statistics may still be collected. Deleting the app removes the records stored on the device.

This app shows Google AdMob ads; standard advertising data such as the Advertising ID may be collected and used by Google and its advertising partners (users in the EEA/UK are shown a UMP consent flow). Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one. The “Remove ads” in-app purchase disables banner and interstitial ads; payments are handled by Google Play and this app never stores payment details. See Google Ads policies.

This app uses Google Firebase Analytics for app usage statistics. Usage events such as app opens and screen views, together with an app instance ID and device information (device model, OS version, approximate country), may be sent to Google. Your study records, such as letters finished, streak and memory album, are not sent. See the Google Privacy Policy.

This app is not directed to children under 13. For questions, contact kitan98@gmail.com.

데이터 보관 / Data retention

Records created by this app are stored only on the user's device. The developer does not receive them on any server and does not retain them. Data kept on the device remains until the user deletes it in the app or uninstalls the app, at which point it is removed with the app. Advertising identifiers used for ads are handled by Google and its advertising partners under their own policies and retention periods; the developer does not retain them.

このアプリが作る記録は利用者の端末内にのみ保存されます。開発者はサーバーで受け取らず、別途保管しません。端末内の記録は利用者が削除するかアプリを削除するまで残り、削除すると一緒に消えます。広告識別子は Google および広告パートナーが各自のポリシーと保存期間に従って扱い、開発者は保管しません。
