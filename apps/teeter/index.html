About this app

Lose balance, lose it all.

It looks like a block puzzle, but there is a scale underneath.
Pieces fall one at a time, and you steer each one onto the left or right pan.

■ Rules
· One piece at a time — move it to either pan
· The farther from the pivot, the more it tilts
· Clearing a line lightens that side and breaks the balance
· When it tilts, the ceiling drops on the lighter side

■ Features
· Clear while balanced to double your score
· Every material weighs differently — wood, stone, iron, gold
· Pieces fall faster as your level rises
· 12 languages, switchable in settings

Sooner or later you will want to clear a line and won't be able to.
That is where this game really begins.
