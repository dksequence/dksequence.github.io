About this app

Radio Waves is an internet radio app where you turn the analog dial of a vintage receiver
to reach 50,000+ real radio stations worldwide, live.
■ Analog tuning — drag the frequency dial to scan between stations on a vintage panel where the
needle sweeps and the meters swing. Pick from 5 radio skins — tube console, field radio, world band receiver, pocket transistor and ham transceiver.
■ Pick a country — choose from 200+ countries and only that country's stations go on the dial.
Each country shows its station count, ⭐ pins the ones you visit often, and your last country is
restored the next time you open the app.
■ Worldwide & live — also browse by genre (pop, jazz, news, lofi, k-pop…), search or top charts,
with the currently playing track shown via metadata. Hit 🎲 to stumble on a station from a far city.
■ Favorites & background — ⭐ saves the station's country and the date you saved it; group favorites
by country or by date and tune a whole country at once. Keep listening from the lock screen, and a
sleep timer stops it when you doze off.
Station lists and streams are provided by public broadcasters and Radio Browser; this app is only a
player. Favorites stay on your device.
Live streaming needs internet (data) and some streams may drop due to the broadcaster. Wi‑Fi
recommended.
