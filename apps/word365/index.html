About this app

A Korean word with a picture, every day.

365 teaches everyday Korean one picture at a time. Each day you get 5 new words, and every word comes with a hand-painted picture, the Korean spelling, how to say it and the English meaning. More than 2000 everyday words wait for you, enough for 409 days of learning.

TODAY'S WORDS
- Five picture cards a day, one tap to the next
- Hear each word (the app uses the Korean voice installed on your phone)
- A streak and a word counter keep you coming back

QUICK QUIZZES
- Pick the picture for the word, or the word for the picture
- Right and wrong answers each get their own sound

TOPICS
- Browse by theme: food, around the house, clothes, animals, nature, transport and more
- Words you have learned light up, the rest wait for their day

REMINDERS
- Choose 0 to 5 reminders a day and set the times
- Each reminder shows one of today's words

YOUR WAY
- Turn the English meaning off to learn from pictures only
- Background music and sound effects can be switched off
- No account needed. Your progress stays on your phone.

Word list: Korean Learner's Vocabulary List, National Institute of the Korean Language (KOGL Type 1).
