About this app

You do not clear the minefield. You cross it alive.

Numbers are only readable under your feet. Every step reveals a little more, and stepping on an empty tile opens up a whole safe area at once.

▪ No need to open the whole board — reach the exit and you are done
▪ Stages 1–25 only deal boards you can cross by logic alone. On bigger boards, use Sniff or Hint when you get stuck
▪ Plant a flag on any tile you suspect
▪ One wrong step ends the run right there. There is no undo
▪ 60 stages across six regions: Meadow, Dusk, Night, Midnight, Snowfield, Ashlands
▪ Boards grow from 9×9 all the way to 128×128
▪ Three stars are about how few tiles you stepped on, not how fast you were
▪ Sniff checks one tile; Hint marks a tile proven safe together with the numbers that prove it

No reflexes required. Sit down and think.
