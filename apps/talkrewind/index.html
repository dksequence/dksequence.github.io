About this app

Sometimes you want to see a message again after you read and deleted it, or after you left the chat.

Talkrewind automatically keeps the message notifications that reach your phone. Even if a message is deleted or you leave the room in the original app, your copy in Talkrewind stays.

WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU
• Sorts messages by app: SMS, Telegram, and more
• Groups each chat room on its own
• Saves the same message only once
• Find anything fast by search or date

YOU CHOOSE HOW LONG TO KEEP
• 15, 30, 60, 90, 150 or 200 days (starts at 90)

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
• Fingerprint or PIN app lock
• 12 languages, with a language picker in Settings
• The remove-ads purchase turns off banner and interstitial ads

GOOD TO KNOW
• Only messages that arrive as a notification are saved. Messages from before install, muted chats, and messages you send are not saved.
• Saved content never leaves your phone.

Talkrewind is not affiliated with Kakao, Meta, Telegram or any messaging company.
