About this app

Some days hurt when you forget them. A parent's birthday, your wedding anniversary, your child's big day. Save those days in Life Alarm and it will tell you ahead of time — thirty days out, a week out, on the morning itself. You choose the days for each one.

■ Features
· Set your own lead times — 30, 7, 1 day before, or the day itself. Up to 8 per day
· Lunar birthdays converted to the right solar date every year
· Age and anniversary count worked out for you
· Soonest first, each card with a relationship badge — parents, partner, children
· Save one line that shows up in the reminder itself
· A reminder time of its own for every day you save
· 12 languages, switchable inside the app
· A quiet background track, off whenever you like
· Optional Remove Ads product
