About this app

Shake it. The cosmos answers.

Cosmic 8 Ball is a 3D oracle ball floating in deep space. Whisper your question, shake your phone (or tap the ball), and watch the answer rise from the abyss of the cosmic glass.

FEATURES
- Real 3D glass ball (Three.js) with nebula, stars and bubble particles
- Shake detection tuned for real hands - or just tap the ball
- All 20 classic answers
- Verdict colors at a glance: positive (mint), hazy (violet), negative (orange)
- 4 oracle realms: Classic Oracle, Stars of Love, Crossroads, and the premium Abyss Oracle
- Oracle log and one-tap share
- Korean / English

ABYSS ORACLE
Watch a rewarded ad to open the deepest realm for 24 hours, or unlock it forever with a one-time purchase that also removes all ads.

NOTE: All answers are for entertainment only - not advice, fortune-telling or predictions.
