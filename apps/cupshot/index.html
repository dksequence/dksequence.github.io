About this app

Throw paper in the trash — the office game everyone has played with a crumpled cup.

HOW IT PLAYS
One flick, three variables:
• Direction — where you swipe
• Power — how far it flies and how fast it tumbles
• Curl — swipe an arc and the cup bends in the air

Snap your wrist and the cup tips back, throwing coffee out in a long ribbon. When the spin cancels the tilt exactly, that is a Perfect.

TEN SPOTS IN ONE OFFICE
My Desk, Pantry, Copy Room, Mail Room, Lounge, Storage, Rooftop, Corner Office, Late Night, Year End Party. Distance, wind, obstacles and the bin itself change at every spot — a throw that works at your desk sails right past the bin on the rooftop.

NINE CUPS TO THROW
Every spot hands you a different cup with its own size, weight, drag and drink inside, so the same swipe never behaves the same way twice.

MORE INSIDE
• 10 different bins, each with its own size and shape
• Endless run — keep throwing until you miss three times
• Miss feedback that tells you why: short, long, left, right, or rim
• Three stars per spot, and the next spot opens as you earn them

BUILT TO BE PLAYED ONE-HANDED
One swipe is the whole control scheme. No buttons on the throwing screen, and no ad banner under your finger while you aim.

12 languages with an in-app language picker.

Supported by ads. A one-time purchase removes banner and full-screen ads.
