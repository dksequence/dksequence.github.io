About this app

Duel Korean chess masters, one board at a time. Janggi Master is a Janggi (Korean chess) game played against an AI that runs on your device.

DOJO CHALLENGE
• Clear 10 dojos in sequence to prove your skill
• Win 10 games in a dojo and its sign splits open to unlock the next one
• A loss doesn't reset your win count

INSTANT PLAY
• Pick any level from 1 to 10 and start a game right away

PIECE TUTORIAL
• See exactly how each of the 7 piece types — General, Guard, Horse, Elephant, Chariot, Cannon, Soldier — moves on the board

MATCH AIDS
• Use Hint, Undo and Insight (a peek at the opponent's likely next move)
• Your win/loss/draw record is tracked

GOOD TO KNOW
• The app shows ads. You can purchase to remove ads or to unlock all levels
• There is no online multiplayer, chat or spectating, and no betting
