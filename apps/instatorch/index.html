About this app

InstaTorch is already on the moment you tap the icon.

■ Why it's faster
Your phone's built-in flashlight takes 2-3 steps: wake the screen, pull down Quick Settings,
tap the tile. InstaTorch is different — the light is already on by the time the app opens.
One action. Tap the icon again to turn it off instantly.

■ Features
· One tap to turn on, one more tap to turn off
· Brightness in steps (on supported devices)
· SOS Morse signal
· Home screen widget & Quick Settings tile — no need to open the app
· The light needs only the camera permission (notification permission is optional, for the "on" notice); your language choice is remembered

This flashlight uses your camera's LED and never asks for location, microphone, or contacts.
