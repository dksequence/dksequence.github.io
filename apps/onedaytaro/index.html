About this app

Every morning, one card for the day. Today's TARO is a small ritual in your hand that quietly tells you how to meet today.
■ Today's Card — once a day, draw your card and receive a gentle "approach today like this" guidance. Renews at midnight.
■ Tarot Reading — pick cards in spreads (past·present·future, situation·action·outcome) for per-position and overall interpretation.
■ Topic Readings — love, work, money, mind, choices, growth — read the flow for your theme.
■ Full 78-card deck — 22 Major + 56 Minor, each hand-crafted in black-and-gold celestial art.
■ Atmosphere — ritual-like shuffle/reveal/flip motion, mysterious ambient music, a starry night sky.
■ The Choice Card (premium) — at a crossroads, put your dilemma into one line and draw a single card for its guidance.
■ On-device readings — no server or account. Your readings never leave your device (ads use the network).

For self-reflection and entertainment.
