About this app

Tilt it and the liquid sloshes for real. Shake it and the foam rises.
DK Beer is a liquid simulation where you pour and sip beer, soju, whisky and wine on one screen.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
• Real liquid physics - it flows where you tilt, splashes, and climbs the glass wall
• Four drinks - beer with a foam head, crystal-clear soju, amber whisky, deep red wine
• Find the best ratio - mix and pour until the blend feels just right
• Pour into a friend's phone - tilt your glass and send the drink to a phone nearby
• A glass that feels cold - condensation beads, sliding drops and slowly spreading frost
• 30 background tracks - shuffled for you (some with vocals)
• 12 languages

HOW TO PLAY
1. Long-press the glass to change drinks, swipe to flip through them
2. Tilt to pour, shake for foam, tilt further to sip
3. In Settings: new glass, start mode (pour from empty / start full) and tilt sensor

GOOD TO KNOW
This is a virtual simulation game and does not encourage real drinking. Adults only, and please enjoy responsibly.
Connecting to a friend's phone needs Bluetooth and nearby-device permissions. Devices connect directly to each other.

The app contains ads. A single optional purchase removes them.
