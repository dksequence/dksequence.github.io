About this app

Screenshots pile up long after you needed them. Sweep pulls only the screenshots from your album and shows them as cards. Swipe left to add one to the clear list, swipe right to keep it. Confirm once at the end and everything you gathered moves to the trash, with the space you freed recorded.

■ Features
· Swipe to gather, confirm just once at the end
· Undo anytime while you sort
· Auto-sorted into payments, shopping, chats and maps
· Pull out account and coupon numbers as text and keep them
· Group near-duplicate captures and keep one
· A monthly record of the space you freed

■ Privacy
Photos and recognized text are processed only on this device and never sent anywhere.
* Deleting photos moves them to the trash through the system dialog and, depending on device settings, they can be restored for a while.
