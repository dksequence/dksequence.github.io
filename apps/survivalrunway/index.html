About this app

Thinking about quitting, switching jobs, or facing a layoff? This calculator turns "can I actually afford to leave right now" into a number. Enter what you have and what goes out each month, and it instantly shows how many months you can last and roughly when the money runs out.

■ Features
· Add cash, savings, emergency funds, and other assets freely to total up what you have
· Enter monthly spending by category (rent, food, phone, insurance...), with optional income such as unemployment benefits
· A gauge and balance-trend graph show your survival months and the expected exhaustion date at a glance
· Simulation sliders show instantly how many extra days you gain by "cutting spending 10%" or "adding $1,000"
· Save or share your results as an image card
· A 7-day check-in streak builds the habit of reviewing your finances regularly
· Every amount you enter is kept only on this device

■ Who it's for
· Anyone weighing whether to quit or switch jobs and wants a head start on the numbers
· Anyone who just lost a job and wants a concrete monthly budget plan
· Anyone who wants to regularly check how many months their emergency fund covers
