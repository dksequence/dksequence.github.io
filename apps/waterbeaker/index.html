About this app

Your phone screen turns into a beaker. Tap and the water rises, tilt the phone and it sloshes along. Reach your daily goal and a cup is added to your shelf.

THE FEEL OF DRINKING
- Tap for one cup, long-press to choose one cup, two cups or your own amount
- Tilt the phone and the water moves; the scale shows how much you have had
- A gulp sound and haptics, plus a 3-second undo right after you log

LOG YOUR DAY AND LOOK BACK
- Set your own daily goal and cup size
- The notebook compares the last 5 days, a week or a month with your goal at a glance
- The day resets at 6 a.m., so late-night logs are not cut off

REMINDERS AND LOOKS
- Drink reminders every 1, 2, 3 or 4 hours (7 a.m. to 10 p.m.), off whenever you like
- 9 skins that you change with a left or right swipe, plus music and sound effects settings
- 12 languages, chosen inside the app

KEPT ON YOUR PHONE
- Your records are stored only on this device and no account is needed

Note: this app logs how much water you drink and is not medical advice. A banner ad appears below the notebook and settings screens, and an interstitial ad occasionally appears when you close the notebook. A one-time purchase removes ads, and you can watch an ad to hide banners for 24 hours.
