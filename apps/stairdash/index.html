About this app

Stair Dash is a fast, addictive arcade climber on never-ending crumbling stairs!

Play a courier loaded with boxes and climb the collapsing staircase one step at a time.
Tap the arrow that matches the next stair — same side to go straight, opposite to turn.
Easy to learn, but the rising speed will keep you on the edge.

■ Juicy action
· Each step you leave crumbles and falls away beneath you
· Your stack of cargo wobbles precariously as you leap
· Rain, lightning, hail and booming thunder build up over time

■ Obstacles — leap over them
Burning, poison, broken, and box-blocked stairs — see one, tap JUMP!

■ 8 characters + coin shop
From the default couriers to a Neon Rider, Ninja, Robot, and Astronaut.
Each carries different cargo (boxes, bottles, glassware) and moves differently.
Collect coins to unlock new characters.

■ Friendly extras
· A no-timer "slow tutorial" to learn the controls
· 6 languages · "Continue" with coins · All records stored only on your device
