About this app

One tap, one point.

Tapboard turns your phone into a big, bright scoreboard for any game. Pick how many players or teams you need, anywhere from 1 to 10, and the screen splits into large colored slots you can read from across the table.

■ How it works
Tap a slot to add a point. Press and hold to take one away. Made a mistake? Undo steps back one move at a time.

■ Name every slot your way
· Numbers: 1, 2, 3 …
· Letters: A, B, C …
· Custom words: type any name, like a player or a team
Names are kept even when you change the number of slots.

■ Made to be heard
Every tap makes a sound, so you know it counted without looking. Choose Click, Tick, Pop, Beep or no sound. Adding and subtracting sound different, so a wrong press is easy to catch. Vibration can be turned on or off.

■ Fits the way you hold it
Hold the phone upright and the slots stack top to bottom. Turn it sideways and they sit side by side for a face-off. Up to ten slots, always in reading order.

■ Details
· Every slot has its own color, up to 10
· Points per tap: +1, +2, +3, +5 or +10
· Scores are saved every time they change
· Keep the screen on while you play
· 12 languages, selectable in the app

For board games, card games, quizzes, party games, sports practice and friendly rivalries.
