About this app

Psybox is a collection of 30 fun psychology tests that reveal the real you. From love style to past life, hidden superpowers, and fantasy race — discover a new side of yourself every time.

Answer 5 simple questions and get an intuitive result with emoji cards and warm interpretations.

■ 30 tests, wonderfully varied
• 💕 Love & relationships — love style, ideal type, friendship style
• 🧠 Personality — hidden color, thinking type, social energy, decision style
• 👑 Your type — leadership, talk style, job aptitude, learning style
• 🦊 Fun & fantasy — animal face, past life, hidden superpower, fantasy race, magic element
• 🌿 Mind & healing — stress animal, healing style, happiness type, anger type
• ✨ Mood — your season, flower, aura color, night sky, life genre
…and many more waiting for you!

■ How it works
• Five popular tests are included to start.
• Unlock the rest by watching an ad, or get Premium (Remove Ads) to open all 30 at once.

■ Features
• On-device — your test answers and results are processed only on your phone.
• Emoji card results.
• Mystical background music and playful sound effects.
• Multi-language (한국어·English·日本語·中文·Español).

Meet the real you and your friends — 30 ways, in Psybox!
