About this app

Math King looks like a game — but inside, it's a serious math trainer. Climb 11 ranks from Beginner (elementary) to Perfect Master (college senior). Solve 50 curriculum-accurate problems per rank to unlock the next one. Arithmetic, equations, functions, calculus, matrices, probability — every problem is generated on your device to match the curriculum, with exact answers and solutions.

■ Features
· 11 ranks from elementary to college
· Curriculum-accurate auto-generated problems
· Clear 50 problems to rank up
· Step-by-step answer explanations
· Rank roadmap, combos & stats
