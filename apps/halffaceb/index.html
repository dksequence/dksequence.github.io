About this app

Is your face perfectly symmetrical? Half of Me splits your face down the middle and mirrors each side, showing you "two faces" from a single photo.

• Symmetry score — measure how symmetrical your left and right sides are
• Left Me & Right Me — each half completed into a full, mirrored face
• Left/right reading — what your emotional (left) and rational (right) sides say about you
• Symmetry analysis — from "amazing symmetry" to "a face full of character"

Take a photo or pick one from your gallery, fit your face in the circle, and you're done. Compare symmetry with your friends!

* 100% just for fun. Photos are processed only on your device.
