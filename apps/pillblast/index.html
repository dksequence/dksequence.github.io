About this app

This is no ordinary brick breaker. The pills are the star.

▶ 20 pills — weapons, paddles, balls, WORLDs
Gatling, laser, bomb ball, homing missile, giant ball, pierce, multiball, magnet, twin paddle… bricks are just vending machines for pills.

▶ Synergy — a fused third effect
Activate the right two pills together and they fuse into something new — 7 combos to find.

▶ World pills — the 'wow'
4D warp twists the whole screen and your ball pierces everything! Gravity flip, black hole, time stop.

▶ To eat or not — risk
Not every pill is good. Debuffs drop in, so every catch is a decision.

A one-handed brick breaker where pills ARE the content. Conquer 10 islands!
