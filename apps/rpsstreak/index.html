About this app

Rock Paper Scissors 500 Streak is a winning-streak challenge: beat 500 rivals one after another with rock paper scissors — or the Korean variant, Muk-jji-ppa.

Your rivals wait in a line and step up one at a time. Throw your hand within the 3-2-1 count as the call rings out. Win and the line moves down to the next rival. The higher your rank, the faster the count and the smarter the rivals.

■ Features
· Two modes: Rock Paper Scissors and Muk-jji-ppa
· 100 ranks × 5 rivals = 500 opponents, with a count that speeds up as you rise
· 500 characters in uniform — animals, bugs, dinosaurs and aliens
· Every rival has a habit — read the hint on the card and counter it
· Muk-jji-ppa keeps both hands on screen as the attack passes back and forth
· Two lives plus one chance to continue
· A Hall of Fame that keeps your best rank as a golden medal card
· Available in 12 languages
