About this app

Focus Master

Number recall, spot the difference, mazes, sliding puzzles, shape matrices, color traps and
rock-paper-scissors inhibition — sixty short brain-training games in one app. Play a few
minutes a day and watch your focus index and rank grow.

■ 60 focus games
· Tracking — shell tracker, multi-object tracking, orbit trail, line trace, maze, slide puzzle
· Selective attention — odd one out, hidden target, spot the difference, shape matrix, mental rotation
· Inhibition — go/no-go, color trap, mirror move, forbidden hand, hold on three
· Working memory — simon says, N-back, digit span, number recall, board match, what vanished
· Divided attention — split focus, double count, peripheral catch, match count
· Reaction — quick tap, flash grab, beat keeper, blind stop, hand orders

■ Two modes
· Career — clear stages one by one and chase three stars
· Endless — one miss and it is over. How far can you go?

■ Focus you can measure
· Every round scored into a focus index (0-100) and a rank
· Six brain skills tracked with a recent-history graph in your profile

■ Easy to start
· Ten games are always included; watch an ad to unlock any locked game for 24 hours
· Buy Remove Ads to unlock all 60 games and remove ads
· no account · records stay on your device

※ A score for fun and training only. It is not a medical test, diagnosis or treatment,
and makes no claim of preventing any disease.
