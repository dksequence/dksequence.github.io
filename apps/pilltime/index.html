About this app

Keep forgetting your medicine? Set the times and days for each medicine, and Pilly reminds you right on time.

■ On-time reminders
· Several times a day per medicine, on the days you choose
· Allow "Exact alarms" and reminders arrive exactly at the time you set
· Your reminders stay scheduled even after the device restarts

■ Act right from the notification
· [Taken] — records the dose without opening the app
· [Remind in 10 min] — can't take it now? Get one more reminder in 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes
· Mark a dose as taken early and that day's reminder won't ring

■ Today's doses
· Everything you need to take today, in time order — tap once to mark it taken
· Past doses are marked "Missed", so you can see right away what you skipped

■ Adherence and streak
· See how consistently you took your medicine over the last 7 days
· Days with every dose taken build your streak (rest days don't break it)

■ Easy to tell apart
· Set a color and dose for each medicine
· 12 languages supported, with a language picker inside the app

■ Good to know
· Your medicines and dose records are saved on this device; no account or login is needed
· You can add up to 5 medicines, and 1 more each time you watch an ad. A one-time "Remove ads + unlimited meds" purchase removes ads and the limit
· Contains ads
· Pilly is a reminder and tracking tool and does not replace medical advice. Follow your prescription and the label

Family medicines, vitamins and supplements, daily pills — take them on time.
