About this app

Archery Tour

A real archery range: a distant target, a scope to aim through, and wind you have to read.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
- SOLO: six arrows, one score. Chase three stars on every stage.
- VERSUS: alternate shots against a rival under World Archery match play rules.
Three arrows a set, two points for the set, first to six points wins.
Five-all goes to a shoot-off, one arrow each, closest to the centre takes it.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Hold to draw. Slide to move the sight. Release when the gauge glows.
- The target is far away, so a magnifying scope shows the rings while you aim.
- The impact marker tells you where the arrow will really land, drop and wind included.
- No random spread. If you aim well, you hit. Skill is aiming and draw control.
- After the shot the camera flies to the target and zooms in on where it landed.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- 40 rounds across 10 host cities, each a different place and season:
cherry blossom in Seoul, an indoor hall in Tokyo, a summer beach in Rio,
autumn leaves in Paris, desert sunset in Mexico City, a floodlit stadium in LA,
sea cliffs in Antalya, pine forest in Berlin, stone ruins in Rome, ice in Helsinki.
- Distances from 18m to 70m, with breeze, steady wind and gusts.
- Eight rivals, from Rookie to Legend.
- A commentator calls every arrow.
- Bows and arrows to collect in twelve colours, bought with coins.
- Knock Down mode: 400 physics rounds of toppling pins, pots and crates.
- 12 languages with an in-app language picker.

Aiming tip: arrows drop as they fly, and the wind moves in a rhythm you can wait out.
