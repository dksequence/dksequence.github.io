About this app

Who's buying coffee? Who does the chores? Ladder Draw decides anything fairly with a cute animated ghost-leg (Amidakuji) game.

■ Delightful — rounded cute design, fun SFX & BGM, winning effects.
■ Anywhere — gatherings, friendly bets, lunch picks, chore duty.
■ 6 languages · local, no login.
