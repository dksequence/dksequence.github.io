About this app

You and your rival face off across a court, tossing bombs back and forth. Clear your side first to win. You can only catch an incoming bomb with an empty hand — if your hands are full, it knocks you down.

■ Features
· 100 stages to clear, one rival at a time
· Pick one of 8 animal fighters and go all the way
· Court theme changes every 10 stages
· Drag to move, tap to toss — one-handed play
· 12 languages, switchable in-app · sound can be turned off
· Optional Remove Ads product
