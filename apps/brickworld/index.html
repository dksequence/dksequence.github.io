About this app

The satisfying feel of shooting balls to smash bricks — now with a world tour and characters.

Pick a backpacking traveler and take on a brick-breaking tour across 10 countries. 100 stages per country, and the final stage is a boss battle against that country's master! Beat them and a stamp slams onto your passport. Collect 10 stamps to become World Champion.

■ What makes it different
· Passport stamp meta — fill your passport as you clear each country
· Landmark bricks — the Eiffel Tower, Pyramids and Great Wall appear as brick patterns
· Country master bosses — every 100th stage is a boss with gimmicks (steel bricks invincible for 3 turns)
· 8 travelers — each with a different passive that shapes your play style
· The final stage is Korea — Gyeongbokgung & Namsan Tower, a showdown with the strongest master

■ Relax and play
· No hearts, no lives — zero stress, play as long as you like
· One-hand aim and fire, easy for anyone
· Play anytime · 12 languages
