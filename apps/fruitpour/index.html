About this app

Pour the juice, sort the colors.

Fruitpour is a calm liquid sorting puzzle. Tap a bottle to pour, and gather each juice color into a single bottle. That is the whole rule — the depth comes from the order you choose.

HOW TO PLAY
- Tap a bottle to pick it up, then tap another to pour.
- Juice only pours onto the same color, or into an empty bottle. A bottle holding a single color can't be poured into an empty one.
- Fill a bottle with four layers of one color to finish it.
- Finish every bottle to clear the level.

WHAT IS INSIDE
- 600 levels, every one of them checked by a solver before it ships. If a level is in the game, it has a solution.
- A difficulty curve that actually rises. Later levels leave you a single spare bottle, where one careless pour really can block you.
- Hidden layers from level 12. A question mark reveals its color only after you pour it.
- Undo any move, restart a level any time.
- A fruit mark on every layer, and a setting to make the marks bolder — color is not the only way to tell layers apart.
- A progress code. Write it down and bring your levels to a new phone.
- 12 languages, chosen right inside the app: Korean, English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Thai, Malay, Indonesian, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil), German, Simplified Chinese.

Removing ads is available as a one-time in-app purchase.
Your progress is saved on your device.
