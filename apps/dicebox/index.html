About this app

A real dice box in your hand — Dice Box 3D!

Tap the screen or shake your phone and the 3D dice tumble, collide and settle naturally inside the box. Not just a random number generator — it's the physical feel of really shaking dice.

■ Lifelike physics
· 3D collisions and spin between dice and walls
· Natural roll-and-settle deceleration
· Impact sounds + haptic vibration

■ Simple to use
· Roll by tap or shake
· Choose 1–4 dice
· Instantly shows each face and the total

■ Premium (PRO)
· Dice colors (black, blue, red, neon), materials (wood, glass, metal), themes (midnight, noir, magenta, neon)
· Remove ads to unlock all PRO — or watch an ad to preview

Board games, TRPG, party games, quick random picks — Dice Box 3D whenever you need dice.
