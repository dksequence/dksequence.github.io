About this app

Russian Roulette is a party game for one phone. It packs three roulette modes to decide who's "it" — who takes the penalty.

■ Three roulettes
① Trigger — load your chosen number of bullets into the cylinder, pass the phone around, and each player taps the screen to pull the trigger. An empty chamber just clicks and you're safe; a live round goes BANG and that player is "it".
② Spin — put the phone in the middle and spin. A metal wheel with everyone's names spins fast, then slows to a stop, and the name under the top pointer is "it".
③ Pistol — put the phone in the middle and tap Spin. A pistol whirls, then slows to a stop, and whoever the barrel points at is "it".

■ Features
· Big, bold visuals that read well across a table or from afar
· Penalty picker — toggle the built-in penalties or add your own
· 2–10 players, no account needed
· A light, stylized party act — no real firearms or violence

Perfect for friends, family, and parties when you need to pick who gets the dare!
