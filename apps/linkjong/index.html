About this app

A mahjong connect puzzle: link two tiles of the same picture to clear them.

[HOW TO PLAY]
- Tap two tiles that show the same picture.
- They clear if the path between them bends no more than twice.
- The path runs through empty cells, and may go one step outside the board.

[FEATURES]
- On 46 of the 60 boards, the tiles shift after every match - down, sideways, to the centre, or apart.
Eight flow directions, plus classic boards that stay still.
- 60 stages, each with three stars and a time limit.
- Runs out of moves? It reshuffles for you. No dead boards.
- Three tools: hint, shuffle and bomb.
- 12 languages, switchable in settings.

Each board lasts two minutes at most - a good fit for a short break.
