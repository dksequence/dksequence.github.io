About this app

Your to-do list keeps growing, but you never see how far you have come.
TreeDo splits a goal into branches and colors the tree as you check things off.

■ How it works
1. Pick what you want to finish (12 starter templates: exams, moving, travel and more)
2. A tree already filled with tasks opens right away
3. Tap what you finished and the color spreads from leaf to root

■ Features
· Partial progress — every branch fills with color as far as you have gone
· Home screen widget — check items without opening the app
· Today view — see what is left without opening the tree
· Weekly record — miss a day and nothing nags you
· Export — image, Markdown, and a finish card
· Safe storage — automatic backup and recovery, deleted items go to the bin

■ Made for
Students preparing for exams, anyone facing a big move or a wedding,
and people whose thoughts do not settle into a flat list.

12 languages supported, and you can pick your language inside the app.
