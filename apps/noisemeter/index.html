About this app

Noise Radar (dB Sound Meter)

A military-radar styled CRT console that measures ambient noise in real time. Audio is analyzed on your device to show an estimated decibel (dB) level and a noise grade, and loud events over 85dB hit the scope as radar blips with an instant alert.

■ Detect like a radar
· Phosphor-green CRT screen with a rotating sweep
· 85dB+ events become target blips with alert sound and haptics

■ Real-time metering
· Live, min, average (Leq) and max dB with elapsed time
· Automatic noise grading in 10 levels (library, conversation, traffic, subway, factory...)

■ Logs & reports
· Auto-save on stop, full graph included
· Grade-LED log with quiet/loud filters
· Session report including threat-event count

■ Tuned to your phone
· ±15dB calibration offset
· PRO precision mode (decimals, L10/L50/L90) — unlock 24h by watching an ad
· Remove-ads in-app purchase

■ Privacy
· Sound is analyzed on-device only — never recorded, never uploaded

* Reference-grade meter. Not a certified instrument; not valid for legal use.
