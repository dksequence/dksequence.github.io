About this app

A note shouldn't disappear the moment you look away. SafeJot needs no save button — it saves the instant you stop typing, and stays there even if you leave the screen or switch apps.

TRUST ENGINE
• Autosave: no save button, no loading spinner, just there
• Version history: every save keeps a prior version you can restore
• 30-day trash: deleted notes wait 30 days before they're gone — restore or delete for good
• Back up to a folder you choose: your notes exported to one file, on a schedule
• Startup integrity check: if something looks wrong, it offers to recover from your last backup

ORGANIZE & FIND
• Color-tag cards for an instant visual sort
• Star ratings and a crown to flag what matters most
• Folders: the home list shows only unfiled notes, "View all" merges everything, and there's a dedicated folder screen too
• Search that highlights exactly where your words appear
• Long-press to multi-select, then bulk delete or move to a folder

EXPRESS YOURSELF
• Insert emoji and colored dividers to break up your text
• Resize the font for one note without touching the rest
• Attach one short voice clip per note (up to 60 seconds) — stored only on your device
• Share the title and body as plain text in one tap

MORE
• Lock any note with biometrics or a PIN
• Two home-screen widgets: jump straight to a new note, or see recent notes
• No account, no cloud sync — everything stays on your device

GOOD TO KNOW
• The app shows ads. You can purchase to remove them
