About this app

A calendar that shows lunar and solar dates together. Pinch to zoom like a map and glide between year, month, week and timeline views.

LUNAR, TERMS, HOLIDAYS
- Korean, Chinese and Vietnamese lunar dates on every day, leap months marked
- 24 solar terms and public holidays for 8 countries
- World observances you can switch on when you want them

EVENTS AND ALERTS
- 15-minute events, all-day and repeating (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, lunar yearly)
- Several alerts per event, snooze right from the notification
- Text color, bold and tags; open a place in your maps app

MAKE IT YOURS
- Crayon, pastel and marker style circles, stars, hearts, pins and emoji
- A memo for any date and a dot view of how your tags are spread
- 10 skins and dark mode, choose the first day of the week, home screen widget

KEPT ON YOUR DEVICE
- Events and decorations are stored on the device, no account needed
- Export and import a backup file
- Optionally read the device calendar (synced Google Calendar) alongside - read only
- 13 languages, switch inside the app

Note: a banner ad appears at the bottom, and a one-time purchase removes it. Notification permission is needed for alerts.
