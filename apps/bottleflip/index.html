About this app

A satisfying physics challenge about flipping a bottle and landing it upright. Flick the screen to control angle and power, spin the bottle through the air, and stick the landing. Clear round after round chasing your best score.

■ Features
· Intuitive one-touch control — angle and power are all that matter
· Adjust difficulty with water fill (Less / Normal / More)
· Roguelike-style run through multiple rounds in a row
· Cozy illustrated backdrop with a calm original soundtrack
· 12-language support

■ Keep your streak alive
· Miss a landing? Watch a short ad to save your streak

※ This app stores only your best score and settings on your device, with no account required.
※ Ads are included. Purchasing "Remove ads" turns them off.
