About this app

Mission Alarm wakes you up for real. To stop the alarm you must solve missions — times tables, 2-digit multiplication, typing today's date, picking the right weekday, retyping a sentence, or a multiple-choice quiz — so you can't half-asleep dismiss it and drift back to sleep.

Why you'll love it
- You've overslept after turning an alarm off without waking up
- You want your brain switched on from the first minute
- You wake at different times on different days

Key features
- 16 mission types + random: times table, 2-digit multiplication, add & subtract, today's date, weekday quiz, sentence and reverse typing, memory digits, find the odd one, tap in order, match pairs, multiple-choice quiz, rock-paper-scissors, shake, walk
- Set 1 to 10 problems per alarm
- Quiz mission restarts from zero if you miss one
- Weekly repeat alarms + quick alarms (in 5, 10, 30, 60 minutes)
- Snooze interval 3/5/10 min with a repeat limit
- 11 alarm sounds, per-alarm volume, vibration, 10s fade-in
- Alarms restored automatically after reboot
- Full-screen alarm over the lock screen

On the days you absolutely must get up, Mission Alarm takes responsibility.
