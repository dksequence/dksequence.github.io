About this app

One morning notification tells you what's being celebrated or observed somewhere in the world today. See international observances, national days, and fun cultural days all in one screen, and mark the ones you like with a check, heart, diamond, or spade to collect them.

■ Features
· A morning notification previews today's occasions
· Covers international days, national days, fun & culture days, and Korean observances
· 4 marks to collect the days you like
· Add your own recurring days (birthdays, anniversaries) and get reminded every year
· Share today's occasion as text
· A gentle background music option

※ The day-by-day content is curated in Korean. The app's menus and buttons support 12 languages.
