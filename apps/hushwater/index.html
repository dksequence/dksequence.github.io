About this app

Ever notice your baby calms down when you turn on the tap? Hush Water started from that moment.

13 SOUNDS
• Running water, hair dryer, vacuum, womb sound, heartbeat, callus remover, handheld vacuum, fan, plastic bag rustle
• 4 lullabies (music box, piano, strings, marimba)

MIXER
• Play several sounds together and set the volume for each one separately

SLEEP TIMER
• Sounds fade out gently and stop once the timer runs out

GOOD TO KNOW
• Pick your language inside the app (12 languages)
• The app shows ads. The remove-ads purchase turns off banner and interstitial ads, and you can also turn ads off for 24 hours by watching a rewarded ad
• The app uses no microphone, camera or other sensitive permissions, and collects no information about your baby
