About this app

A ledger for the money and gifts exchanged at life's big occasions. Record what you received and gave at weddings, funerals, first-birthday parties and more, and the app automatically groups everything by person so you can see at a glance who you still owe.

■ Features
· Add entries quickly, one event at a time ("Save & next person")
· Automatic balance per person (received minus given)
· Record flowers-or-gift-only entries with a 0 amount
· Money from family guests is automatically excluded from your own total
· Export and import as a spreadsheet (CSV)
· Fingerprint or passcode lock
· 12-language support

■ Your records, protected
· A separate copy of the last 14 days is kept automatically every day
· Move everything to a new phone with a single backup file
· Deleted entries stay in Trash for 30 days

※ This app stores your records only on this device, with no account required. To move to a new phone, use the backup file yourself.
※ Ads are included. Purchasing "Remove ads" turns them off.
