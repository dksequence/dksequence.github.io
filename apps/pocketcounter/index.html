About this app

Have you ever lost count because you had to look at the screen?
Pocket Counter is built so you never have to.

■ Tap anywhere
One big number, nothing else. Tap anywhere on the screen and it goes up, and fast taps are never dropped.

■ Count with the volume keys
Volume up adds, volume down subtracts. Your fingertips do the counting.

■ Lock mode
Lock the screen and every touch is ignored. Put the phone in your pocket and keep counting with the volume keys.

■ In and out
Tap arrivals and departures separately to see how many are inside right now, with the running total kept alongside.

■ Goals that tell you
Set a capacity or a goal, watch the bar fill and feel a buzz the moment you reach it.

■ Many counters, in groups
Make one for people, items or scores and sort them into groups with totals. Choose a step of 1, 2, 5, 6, 10 or 12 to count stock by the box.

■ History and export
See counts by hour and by day, then export a CSV and open it in your spreadsheet.

■ 12 languages
Pick your language right in the settings.

Queues, door counts, meetings, inventory, scoreboards. Counting should be simple.
