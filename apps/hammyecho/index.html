About this app

Say something! A cute hamster listens to your voice and repeats it back in a funny, high-pitched voice. While you talk, its mouth moves, its ears perk up and it blinks along.

■ Features
· Press and talk — the hamster repeats it right away (up to 30 seconds)
· Keep your favorite voices in 5 saved slots
· Turn a clip into an mp4 video and share it with friends
· Pick from 6 characters and 10 backgrounds
· Every character has its own voice — squeaky, deep, even an echo
· 12-language support

■ Try it
· Sing a line, laugh, or say something silly and watch the reaction
· Save the funny moments as a video and send it in your messenger

※ The microphone permission is needed to record your voice. Recordings are processed and saved on your device only and are never sent anywhere.
※ A one-time purchase removes ads. Watching a rewarded ad turns ads off for 24 hours.
