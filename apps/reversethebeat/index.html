About this app

Hear a familiar melody from the last note to the first. Reverse The Beat reads a photo of sheet music, turns it into notation you can edit, and plays the piece in reverse.

Start from a photo of a score
• Take a picture of sheet music with your camera, or pick an image from your gallery.
• The app reads the score on your device and converts it into editable notation.
• Reading can be wrong. Fix any misread note in the Fix notes editor.
• Play the corrected score backward right away.

Flip your own music files
• Load a music file from your phone and play the whole thing in reverse with Song.
• Turn a 30-second single-line melody into notation and play that part backward too.

Classical library
• A library of public domain classical pieces is built in, such as Fur Elise.
• Mirror measure by measure, left-right (↔) or up-down (↕).
• Shuffle 1 and 2 mix flips into automatic playback.
• Choose a piano sound or a MIDI orchestra sound.
• Keyboard, waveform and harmony spectrum views, plus 4 skins.

Playback controls
• Play, pause and stop buttons
• Tap a measure to continue playing from there.

Privacy
Photos and music are processed on your device and are not sent to any server.

Notes
• Use only scores and music that you own or have the right to use. Copyright responsibility rests with the user.
• The app screens are in English.
