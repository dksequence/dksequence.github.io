About this app

Can I hang the laundry today? Wash the car? Will the sunset be worth watching?

WeatherScore does not just list numbers. It tells you what today's conditions are actually good for.

■ Today's index
· Laundry — real drying power from vapour pressure deficit and wind, not humidity alone
· Car wash — not "is it dry now" but "will it stay clean for the next 48 hours"
· Walk — apparent temperature, wind and UV together
· Sunset — low, mid and high cloud read separately, because that is what colours the sky
· Photo — cloud cover, mist and wind together

■ Forecast scorecard
Every weather app says it is accurate. Few of them prove it.
WeatherScore records its own forecast when you open the app, and the next time you open it, compares yesterday's forecast with what actually happened,
and publishes the score inside the app.
· "Last 30 days · temp error 1.2° · rain hit 84%" — for your neighbourhood
· If the score looks bad, we still show it
· Records stay on your device and are never sent to a server

■ All the basics, too
· Current conditions · 24-hour hourly · about 10 days of daily forecast
· City search · favourites
· Severe weather alerts (United States)
· 12 languages, switchable inside the app at any time

■ We do not hide where the data comes from
Official data from MET Norway and NOAA/NWS,
with the source and the timestamp always shown at the bottom of the screen.

We do not promise to be right. We show you how right we were.
