About this app

Stuck halfway through your cube and not sure what to turn next?

Twistcam is a cube solver that reads your 3×3 puzzle cube through your phone's front camera. It draws arrows on the cube you see on the screen and shows you the solve step by step, one move at a time.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Scan the six faces of your cube
2. Check the colors and fix any square that looks wrong
3. Follow the arrows and turn the cube one move at a time

SCAN ALL SIX FACES
• Line up each face with the square on the screen
• The shot is taken for you when the face fits, or tap the button
• Colors are read for you
• Tap any square that was read wrong to fix it

SOLVE STEP BY STEP
• Twistcam works out a solution for the cube you scanned
• An arrow on the live camera view shows your next move
• After you turn, the camera checks the front face
• When it matches, the next move comes up on its own, so you tap less
• Back-face turns do not change the front face, so tap [Next] for those

LOST YOUR PLACE?
• Scan the cube as it is now
• Twistcam carries on guiding you from there

TIMER MODE
• Get a scramble to mix up your cube
• Press and hold, then let go to start. Tap to stop
• Best time, average of 5 and average of 12

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
• Pick from 12 languages inside the app
• Calm background music that you can turn off in Settings
• The app shows ads. The remove-ads purchase turns off banner and interstitial ads

GOOD TO KNOW
• Made for the 3×3 cube only
• Camera video is used only to read your cube. It is not saved or sent
