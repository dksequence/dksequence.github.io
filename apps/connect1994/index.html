About this app

The modem sings, the blue screen comes up, and something asks for your name.

■ Connecting is the game
The app does not start. It connects.
The dial tone, the tones of the number, the handshake, CONNECT 14400,
and then a second and a half where nothing happens while it finds the host.
That silence is in here too.

■ A server that closed in 1994
You have connected to a server that stopped running thirty years ago.
Twenty-four names are still in the user list.
They do not answer. They are exactly as they were last recorded.
What happened that night is something you piece together from what they left.

■ The locks are understanding, not items
Tapping the chips is enough to walk through the story.
But there are 124 more actions that never appear on a chip.
Only someone who read the room, matched the handwriting on a note,
and counted their own steps can type them in. The key is in your head.

A marker tree says "stone." A camp notebook says "I always started counting at the well."
A slip of paper reads "NNE, E, SSE."
→ Walk it from the well, counting. A room that is on no map opens.

■ How much there is
· 8 parts, 44 chapters, 80 rooms, 313 actions (124 of them hidden)
· Rebuild twenty-four people from their traces, and find the erased twenty-fifth name
· 20-40 minutes a day, about a month of it
· Each new day you play, one more memory comes back

■ The screen
ANSI 16 colours and a fixed-width font, nothing else.
The connect screen is blue, the world is black inside a blue frame, and there is exactly one photograph in the app.

■ Said plainly
Everyone in this world is a program.
You are the only one connected. You will not meet or talk to another player.
