About this app

Pick a song, pick a level, then pick one of 10 rhythm games. The same track feels completely different in every game.

■ 10 games
· Runner — Watch the top half, jump in the bottom half
· Orbit Hop — Tap when the orbiting planet reaches the tile
· Dodge — Drag to dodge the attacks
· Scan Line — Tap as the sweeping line passes
· Woofer Ring — Tap where the note meets the rim
· Swaying Line — Tap the notes on the swaying line
· Lanes — 4 lanes · hold notes
· Arrow Step — Flick in the arrow’s direction
· Beat Mix — Tap the matching pad when the block hits the line
· Beat Stairs — Left or right on every beat

■ Features
· 80 songs across synthwave, K-pop dance, drum and bass, trap, taiko fusion and more
· 10 difficulty levels on every song, from GUM to GOD
· Clear a song to earn a grade and keep your record
· Save your play as a 9:16 vertical video (SHORT)
· Sync calibration for your device: tap to the beat and it adjusts automatically
· Available in 12 languages
