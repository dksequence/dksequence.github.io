About this app

Times Bomb

Light the fuse and pass the phone around. On your turn, answer the times-table question to pass it on — miss it and the fuse burns 3 seconds faster. The fuse length is random every round, so no one knows when it blows. Whoever holds the phone at the blast becomes the star of the penalty roulette. Playing solo? In Survival mode every correct answer burns the fuse faster — chase your best score.

■ Features
· Pass-the-phone party mode (2-8 players)
· Solo survival mode
· Easy / Normal / Hard times tables
· Penalty roulette for whoever holds the bomb
· Random fuse length every round
· 12 languages supported
· no account needed

* A party game for fun. Keep the penalties friendly — the kind everyone can laugh about.
