About this app

Lose focus counting reps during pushups?
Pushup Counter detects each rep from the shadow your body casts as you go down — read by the phone's built-in light sensor when you place it on the floor under your chest. No camera, no permissions. It counts out loud so you can focus on the workout, not the screen.

• Hands-free auto count — detects down-and-up from your body's shadow (no camera, no permissions)
• Counts out loud — hear every rep without looking at the screen
• Free · Goal · Sets modes — target reps, number of sets, rest timer between sets
• "Ding" cue on the way down — instantly know your depth was registered
• Workout background music, vibration & sound feedback, Korean/English voice
• Track your growth — today's reps, streak, best set, last-7-days graph
• Workout history is saved on your device

[How to get the best count]
1. Place the phone on the floor, screen facing up.
2. The sensor sits at the TOP of the phone (near the front camera) — put that top edge under your chest so your body passes right over it. (The detection point is the top edge, not the center of the screen!)
3. Use it where it isn't too dark — it reads your shadow, so some light is needed.

At home, the gym, or traveling — build your pushup routine with just your phone.

* Warm up first and don't overdo it. Stop if you feel pain.
