About this app

A pendulum metronome in wood and brass.

■ A beat that does not drift
Beats are built from audio sample counts, not from a system timer. Even when the screen is busy, the spacing between clicks stays put.

■ Your practice logs itself
Time spent, bars played and the tempo range you used are collected day by day, along with your practice streak.

■ Practice report as PDF
Export the log as an A4 report with student and teacher signature lines, ready to hand in at a lesson.

■ What practice actually needs
· Tap tempo — set the speed by tapping
· 11 time signatures — 2/4 3/4 4/4 5/4 6/4 7/4 3/8 6/8 7/8 9/8 12/8
· Strong, weak or silent set per beat
· Subdivisions — eighths, triplets, sixteenths
· Silent bar trainer — silent bars after sounding ones, to train your inner clock
· Presets

■ 12 languages
You can pick the language inside the app.

For piano, guitar, drums, wind instruments and voice.
