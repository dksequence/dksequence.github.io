About this app

Element Step helps you learn the periodic table in small steps instead of all at once.

Each stage teaches you a few new elements with a card first: the name, the category, the period and group, and a map showing where it sits on the table. Then quizzes repeat them until they stick. Elements from earlier stages keep coming back so you do not forget them.

■ Features
· All 118 elements, learned step by step from the first 5 up to 118
· 20 stages × 30 levels = 600 levels
· Symbol → Name, Name → Symbol and Number → Symbol quizzes
· Find the spot: tap where an element sits on the table
· Tap in order of atomic number, and a rhythm chant that keeps the beat
· Timed boss levels at levels 10, 20 and 30 that mix everything
· 3 hearts, and one star for each heart left
· A periodic table in 10 category colors — zoom, pan, tap an element for its card
· Reads element names aloud using your device's voice
· Available in 12 languages

Try just one stage today and climb toward the whole table.
