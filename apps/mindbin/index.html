About this app

Regrets you never voiced, habits you want to erase, the parts of yourself you dislike — write them here and seal them away.
Once sealed, not even you can open it for 7 days. Your words are encrypted on your device and never sent anywhere.
This isn’t a place to be answered. It’s a place to let go.

• Two-layer seal — a passcode + 7 days of time
• Real encryption · (your content is never sent)
• A red sealing ritual and your own quiet vault
• After 7 days: seal again, or release it forever
• Available in 8 languages

* Sealing uses Seal Tapes; refill by watching an ad or purchasing.
