About this app

Life is finite. My Life Time shows the time you have left in real time —
hours, minutes, seconds — and reminds you each day what to fill it with.

■ Two moods, two worlds
· Positive — your remaining time as “time to fill with happiness.”
· Negative — as “the time you exist on Earth,” every second precious.
One toggle changes the mood, colors, messages, and motion entirely.

■ Today’s Meaning — time turned into experiences
See your time as concrete experiences: great novels finished, calls with your
parents, spring mornings (400+ messages, including a Science set).

■ Your life in numbers
Meals left, springs, Christmases, glasses of water, heartbeats, breaths.

■ Bucket list
Write “Someday I want to…” and check items off as you make them real.

■ Dead‑Time receipt
Turn wasted time into a receipt — “keep this up daily and ○○ hours of your
life evaporate.”

■ More
· Life‑cycle rainbow ring + bar · 6 languages · daily reminder ·
all data stays on your device.

Today never comes back. Face your time — now.
