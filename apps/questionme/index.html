About this app

Sit facing each other. One phone is all you need.

Question Me splits the screen so two people each get their own side. Hold the phone upright and it divides top and bottom; turn it sideways and it divides left and right. The top half is rotated 180 degrees, so the person across from you reads it the right way up.

■ How it works
Tap Start and a spinner bounces between the two sides before landing on one. The question opens on that person's side. Answer out loud. Tap Answered and the next question appears on the other side.

This is a spoken game. No typing, no accounts, no matching with strangers. You play it with the person already sitting in front of you.

■ Twenty stages, six hundred questions
First Spark · This or That · Food Talk · Little Me · Funny Moments
Little Habits · Wanderlust · What You Watch · What If · My Day
People Around Me · Family Stories · Work and Money · First Impression · Our Moments
Honest Feelings · What Hurt · Butterflies · What Comes Next · Late Night

It starts light and gets deeper as you go. Finish a stage and the next one opens.

■ Couple mode and Friends mode
The questions shift depending on who you are playing with. Switch anytime in Settings; each mode keeps its own progress.

■ Good for
· A quiet night with someone you have known for years
· Two people who are just getting close
· A friend you have not seen in a while
· An evening where the conversation has run out

■ Also
· Twenty background tracks, shuffled
· Twelve languages, selectable inside the app
· A skip button, because some questions are hard

Two people who thought they knew each other still have something left to ask.
