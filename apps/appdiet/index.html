About this app

App Diet shows how much storage your install-and-forget apps are hogging, and helps you declutter.
■ Reclaimable storage — a big number for the space used by apps you haven't opened in over a month, so
you know exactly how much space you can get back. ■ Sorted by waste — apps unused the longest and largest first,
with last-used date (e.g. "32d ago") and size, color-coded as almost-never / rarely / in-use. Sort by
waste, unused, size or name. ■ Lighten up — tap Uninstall to jump straight to Android's own uninstall
screen. Set the unused threshold (14/30/60/90 days) and toggle system apps.
All analysis stays on your device; your installed-app list and other apps' usage history are never sent off the device. The app
never uninstalls anything for you — removal always goes through Android's own dialog.
Reading last-used and size needs a one-time "Usage access" grant. It does not use the sensitive
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.
