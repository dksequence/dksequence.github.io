About this app

Just enter team names and MatchSheet builds your tournament bracket and league standings automatically. Convenient for table tennis, badminton, and other tournament and full round-robin league use. Supports up to 30 teams with automatic byes. Tap to pick a winner and it carries through to the next round; for leagues, just enter scores and rankings update automatically.

■ Features
· Single-elimination tournament (2–30 teams, automatic byes)
· Full round-robin league (up to 20 teams, automatic standings)
· Tap to pick a winner — auto-advances to the next round
· Export as image or PDF to print or share
· No login, no subscription — start right away
