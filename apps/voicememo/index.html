About this app

Don't stop to take notes. Just talk, and it becomes text.

Good ideas vanish while you type. Murmur Voice Memo is an ultra-light voice memo pad: press one big button, speak, and your words turn into text that stacks up neatly in time order.

■ This simple
· One button — press and talk. Live dictation appears as you speak.
· Thought timeline — memos are grouped by today, yesterday and date.
· Edit anytime — tap a memo to refine, copy or delete it.
· Keyboard too — type directly where speaking isn't an option.
· Export all — copy every memo as text in one tap.

■ Local · private
· Memos are saved only on your device and left out of device backups. No account, no login, and the app never uploads your memos.
· Speech recognition is handled by your device's speech service, which may use the network depending on your device and settings.

An idea while driving, a task that pops up on a walk, one line before sleep — just say it, and it's written.
