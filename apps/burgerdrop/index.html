About this app

Burger ingredients drift down from the sky.
Read the order card and catch **only what it asks for**.

■ Letting things fall is harder than catching them
Wrong ingredients drop too. Step aside and let them pass.
Burnt patties, wilted lettuce, napkins, ice cubes - one bad catch and the customer stops waiting.

■ Where you catch it becomes the center of gravity
An ingredient does not vanish into a score. It becomes a layer of your burger.
Catch everything on the left and the tower leans left until it topples.
Catch once on the other side and the balance comes back. Catch dead center and the whole stack straightens up.

■ One axis, one thumb
Put a finger anywhere and slide left or right.
Move too fast and the tower sways - dodging has a price.
Shake quickly side to side to knock the top layer off.

■ From one truck to the master kitchen - 10 shops
Start with a street burger truck, work through the toast stand, the deli and the craft
burger workshop, and finish at the master kitchen.
Each shop adds two new ingredients and one new rule:
double patties, sauce precision, spinning drops, wind, a customer who changes their mind.

■ Three ways to play
Shop Run - work from the truck up to the master kitchen.
Endless Kitchen - customers keep coming. How far can you go?
Today's Orders - three orders, new every day. Build your streak.

■ Quick orders back to back - three stars to chase
Accurate catches multiply your points, and perfect ones build a combo.
Playing carefully scores higher than playing fast.

12 languages. Portrait, one hand.
