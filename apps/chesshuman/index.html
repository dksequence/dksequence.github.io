About this app

Chess Human

Not chess where you pick a difficulty — chess where you beat the AI, one by one.

Choose one of 8 human heroes and enter the AI empire. From Lv1 Chessman to the Lv30 Legend, defeat 30 AI bosses in turn and prove the greatness of humankind.

■ 30-stage gauntlet
· 30 AI bosses (Lv1–30) that get genuinely stronger as you climb
· Break through Lv20 for the Master title; defeat Lv30 for the ending

■ Human-like AI
· A pure custom engine (opening book, full rules: castling, en passant, promotion)
· Each level differs in search depth, judgment wobble and blunder rate — beginners can win, experts get a real challenge
· Every boss has a personality (aggressive, defensive, tactical, and more)

■ Features
· 8 human heroes
· Watch a rewarded ad to earn Insight points (up to +3 Insight per game)
· Remove ads / unlock all via in-app purchase
· Available in 12 languages
