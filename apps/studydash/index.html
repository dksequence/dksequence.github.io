About this app

Study Timer measures your study time on a gauge. Press start and the gauge begins to move gently, so you can see at a glance how long you have studied.

■ Two modes
· Elapsed mode — counts up from the moment you start
· Set-time mode — counts down from the time you choose

■ How it works
· Start, pause, stop and reset — touch the screen and the controls appear in the center, then fade away. Most of the time you just see the gauge
· When you stop, the session is saved to your records
· The start time is saved and the elapsed time is calculated from it, so the time keeps going even if the app is closed
· When a set-time timer ends, you get a notification even if you have left the app
· An option keeps the screen on while you study

■ 15 gauge skins
· 15 skins in precise, mysterious and warm moods — all of them keep moving subtly
· 9 skins, such as Sleep Clock, RPM Gauge and Candle Amber, are available from the start
· 6 premium skins, such as Aurora Liquid and Retro Wall Clock, can be tried for 24 hours after watching an ad, or unlocked permanently with a one-time "Remove ads" purchase
· One button on the timer screen switches to the next skin

■ Fewer distractions
· Silent mode — turns off the app's sounds and vibration
· Do Not Disturb — once you allow "Do Not Disturb access", the app turns it on and off for you
· Airplane mode — opens the system settings screen (the app does not switch it on by itself)

■ Records
· See today's study time, your total and the last 7 days on one screen
· Back up and restore by copying and pasting your records

■ Good to know
· Records stay on this device only; no server account or login is needed
· Ads appear on the records and settings screens; ads are not shown on the timer screen
· You allow the notification and Do Not Disturb permissions yourself, only when you use those features
· 12 languages are supported, and you can choose the language inside the app
