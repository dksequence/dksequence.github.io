About this app

This is not a colouring game. It is a forging game.

■ Forging is painting
Pick a tile and forge it. Success deepens the colour; failure fades it.
Push a tile to level 9 and it turns to crystal, and it glimmers.

■ The same pattern rarely comes out the same
A big success bleeds colour into neighbouring tiles. The same marbling rarely comes twice.
(줄 삭제)

■ Materials decide the palette
Amber, sapphire, rose quartz, jade, gold, charcoal. The material you pick sets
which way the colour can drift. You decide the shape; luck decides the texture.

■ Seal it when you like it
There is no completion check. You decide when to stop.
A sealed piece hangs in a frame, signed with your forge count, failures and crystals.

■ We do not hide the odds
· Odds audit - the app compares the odds it showed you against what actually happened.
· Seed verification - the result is fixed before you forge. See this run's promise (SHA-256)
any time in Settings > Verify odds, and past seeds once a piece is sealed.

■ Points fuel the forge
Open the app and take your daily points. Open it on a streak and take more.
Need more? You choose to watch a rewarded ad yourself.

12 patterns - 6 materials - 5 frames - 12 languages
You can change the language inside the app at any time.
