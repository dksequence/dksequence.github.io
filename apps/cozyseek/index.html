About this app

Slow down and search a warm, watercolor-style world.

Six regions, sixty scenes. Every scene is a watercolor illustration packed with objects — find the ones on your list, one by one. And in every single scene, a little white cat named Cozy is hiding somewhere.

- 6 regions × 10 stages = 60 watercolor-style scenes (Sunlit Lane, Song of the Waves, Whispering Woods, Snowflake Lullaby, Night of Lanterns, Starlight Terminus)
- Find the hidden mascot Cozy in every scene
- No timers, no pressure — search at your own pace
- Fewer misses, more stars
- Hints for when you get stuck
- Remove ads with a one-time purchase
- Available in 12 languages

A quiet walk through a picture, whenever you need one.
