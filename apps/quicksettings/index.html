About this app

Quick Settings is a shortcut maker that spares you the trip through deep settings menus. Pick the settings you open most, give them your own icon, and place them on your home screen — one tap opens that settings screen.

■ Features
· 30+ settings screens as shortcuts: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, airplane mode, brightness, battery saver, language and more
· Style the icon: shape, color and symbol (icon, emoji or text)
· Name it yourself and check the preview before adding it
· See everything you made in My shortcuts
· The list shows only settings that actually open on your device
· 12 languages with an in-app language picker

Note: some settings may be missing, and icon shapes may look different, depending on your device and launcher. Adding to the home screen requires a launcher that supports pinned shortcuts.
