Puzzle Only the crayon in your hand can pass. Draw the path, print it too.

About this app

A maze printed on paper, solved by drawing with crayons.

The paths have colors. You can only cross a path that matches the crayon you are holding,
and black paths are always open. The crayon you need is lying somewhere in the maze.
That is why this maze cannot be solved by scanning it with your eyes — you have to work out
which crayon to fetch first.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Draw without lifting your finger. Backtrack and the mark is erased.
- Step on a crayon and you pick it up. You hold one at a time.
- A gate of another color will not let you through. Every color carries its own pattern,
so it still reads if colors are hard to tell apart.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT HERE
- Every board shipped here was solved end to end by the generator before it went in.
- No timer, no lives. Stay on a board as long as you like.
- Export any maze to an A4 PDF and solve it on paper, answer sheet included.
- Twelve languages, switchable inside the app at any time.

DIFFICULTY
Boards get harder as the number of crayon swaps grows. Later books also use bigger mazes and more crayon colors.
