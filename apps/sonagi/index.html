About this app

The sky darkens. Attack cards pile up and the storm keeps growing.
Fail to counter it now, and the whole shower lands on you.

Sonagi is a card duel played with our own original deck, the Sky Deck.
Four skies — sun, rain, snow, and wind — and twelve characters fill every round.

■ Counter-attacks — the heart of the game
When an opponent plays an attack card, you can strike back with an equal or stronger one and pass an even bigger storm along.
Fail to block it and you take the entire pile. The sky goes dark, lightning cracks — “Sonagi!”

■ Two shouts
Shout “Sonagi!” when you counter a storm, and “Clearing!” when you are down to your last card.
Every climax has its moment.

■ Ways to play
· AI matches — opponents with distinct personalities, from laid-back to sharp-timed.
· Tournament tables grow as you climb — from 1-on-1 up to 4 seats: you plus 1 to 3 AI opponents.
· Tournament — beat 20 opponents one by one and climb to the top.

■ The Sky Deck and its cast
Heralds, guardians, and monarchs — twelve characters live on the cards.
Cards fly from your hand to the pile, and your rivals talk back.

■ Play it your way — rule presets
Tune the role of the Ace, the last-card restriction, the number of storm cards, the counter-attack range, and your starting hand.
Bring in the house rules you grew up with.

■ Feel every play
Lightning and thunder, pouring rain, confetti on victory, and 20 background tracks.

An original card game that wraps the familiar one-card (Crazy Eights) rules in a brand-new Sky Deck.
Start your first round now.
