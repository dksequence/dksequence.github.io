About this app

One silver ball, six cities. Change the city and the whole table changes with it.

Seoul is a four-flipper classic. Tokyo is a cup table. Mumbai is a bank of standup targets. Cairo runs a spiral pipe past a spinning disc and a magnet. Paris pushes the ball uphill on a conveyor ramp. Moscow is a grid of numbered targets. Six cities, six completely different machines — flipper count and position, scoring rules and signature devices all differ.

Draining is not the end. Fall past the flippers and you land on the basement save floor, where you have eight seconds to knock the ball into a return chute and get back to the main table. One chance per ball.

Features
- 6 cities, 6 tables — different flipper layouts and different scoring rules
- A basement save floor gives you one more chance after every drain
- Match three mission cards for a score multiplier up to 10x
- Spinning discs, magnets, conveyors and scoops as city signature devices
- Unlock by mastering a high score, by collecting points, or with a single purchase
- 12 languages, switchable inside the app
