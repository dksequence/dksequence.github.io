About this app

A speedcubing timer built for practice. Rest both hands on the pads at either side of the screen, wait for red to turn green, lift one hand to start and tap the screen to stop. Get the feel of a dedicated hardware timer with nothing but your phone.

■ Features
· Scrambles for 6 events: 3x3 (random-state scrambles), 2x2, 4x4, 5x5, Pyraminx and Skewb
· Optional 15-second inspection: beeps at 8 and 12 seconds, +2 after 15 seconds, DNF after 17
· Stats: best, mean, ao5, ao12 and ao100 with a progress graph
· Solve management: +2 / DNF / delete, split sessions, copy as CSV
· 1v1 duel: put the phone between two people sitting face to face, each on their own pads, first to 3 wins
· 10 screen skins: Carbon Hardware, Brutal Pop, Brutal Stripe, Brutal Mint, Holo Glass, Cyber Esports, HUD Dashboard, Pastel Fluid, Ink Hanji, Retro Handheld
· Settings: ready delay (instant / 0.3 / 0.5 / 1.0 s), hide the time while solving, sound effects, vibration
· 12 languages with an in-app language picker
· Your solves and settings stay on your device, and no account or sign-in is needed

Note: vibration strength and feel vary by device. Ads appear at the bottom of the Home and Records screens and after you leave a screen, never during a solve or a duel. A one-time Remove Ads purchase is available.
