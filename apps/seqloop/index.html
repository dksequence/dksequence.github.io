About this app

Continuously play DKsequence's healing music albums through a nostalgic cassette-deck screen. Each long video holds multiple songs (chapters) you can skip between individually, with shuffle and repeat support. Sit back and enjoy the music on a warm, brushed-metal player.

■ Features
· Continuous album playback
· Jump between chapters (songs) within a video
· Shuffle and repeat modes
· Cassette-deck styled player screen
· 12-language support

※ This app streams music via YouTube. An internet connection is required, and depending on the video, YouTube sign-in may be requested. Data usage applies — Wi-Fi is recommended.
