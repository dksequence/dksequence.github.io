About this app

Swipe up, down, left or right to slide the tiles and merge matching numbers. The rules are simple, but every move counts more as the board fills up.

■ Features
· Three boards - Speed 3x3, Classic 4x4, Marathon 5x5
· A separate best score for each board
· Three undos, refill with a rewarded ad (optional)
· Progress saves itself, continue any time
· Smooth tile animation and calm background music (can be turned off)
· 12 languages
