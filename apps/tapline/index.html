About this app

At the end of the day, Tapline tells you how much you actually used your phone.

■ Touched time vs. screen-on time
Time with the screen lit is not the same as time you actually used it. Video left playing,
a screen that simply stayed on — those stretches are shown separately. Tapline uses your
phone's screen timeout as the reference and never asserts what happened; it shows you the
stretch and lets you judge. The threshold is yours to adjust.

■ Every hour at a glance
A 24-hour chart shows when you used it most. Night hours (23:00-06:00) are marked
separately, so late-night scrolling is easy to spot.

■ Your week
Compare the last 7 days on one screen and see which day it piled up.

■ By app
See exactly where your time went, app by app.

■ Nightly and weekly reports
Not just a number. Tapline tells you how much it changed from yesterday and which app
drove the change. You choose the time and the day.

■ Home widget
Check today's usage and the hourly chart from your home screen, without opening the app.

■ 12 languages
한국어 · English · 日本語 · 繁體中文 · 简体中文 · Español · Français · Deutsch ·
Português (BR) · Bahasa Indonesia · Bahasa Melayu · ไทย
Switch any time inside the app.

■ Permissions
Usage access, which you turn on yourself in Settings, and notification permission for the nightly and weekly reports.
Tapline never reads your screen. It only sees which app was used, when, and for how long.
No accessibility service is requested.
