About this app

The screen is cut in half: the grass above, the soil below.
You can watch the moles digging their tunnels.

■ Not a guessing game
Every whack-a-mole shows you holes and nothing else. Tap when something pops up. That is all.
Molewick shows you underground. Read which hole a mole is heading for, watch it swerve,
and put your finger there before it arrives.

■ Six moles, six ways to read them
· Digger - you can see it tunneling. Predict its hole
· Diver - never appears underground. It just bursts out
· Faker - passes the hole, or climbs halfway and changes its mind
· Swarm - comes up the same hole one after another
· Miner - never surfaces. Tap it underground to stop it
· Veteran - avoids the holes you keep hitting

■ 400 levels, seven strata
From topsoil down through clay, gravel, shale, mineral and fossil to the ember layer.
20 stages, 20 levels each. The deeper you dig, the faster they get.

■ Made for you if
· Pure reflex games feel shallow to you
· You want a game you can play one round at a time
· 12 languages, switchable any time inside the app
