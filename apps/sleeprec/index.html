About this app

You are the one person who never gets to see yourself asleep.

■ Keeps recording with the screen off
No need to leave the screen on all night. Put the phone by your head, turn the screen off, and it keeps going.

■ Tells you what time each sound happened
What you said at 03:12. What you did at 05:40. Instead of one long file, only the moments with sound are kept.

■ Eight hours in a handful of cards
You do not have to listen to all of it. Only the moments with sound become cards, and one tap plays that moment.

■ Night score
One number for the whole night. It compares to yesterday, and on a quiet night you get the most beautiful sky.

■ Adapts to your room
Busy street, fan running - the threshold adjusts to the background on its own. You only pick one of three sensitivity steps.

■ Recordings stay on this phone
Audio is stored on the device and is not sent to a server.

This app is not a medical device and cannot be used to diagnose or treat any condition. Records are for reference only.
