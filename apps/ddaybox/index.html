About this app

There is always a day you are waiting for. A trip, an exam, the day a child was born.
Countdown Box puts that date on your home screen and counts the days for you.

■ Home screen widget
The day you pin shows up large in the widget. You see it the moment you unlock the phone.
The number moves on its own when the date changes, and tapping it opens the app.

■ A reminder that morning
Choose the day itself, the day before, or a week ahead (D-7). One notification at 9 AM.
Nothing goes off in the middle of the night. You can turn it off any time in settings.

■ Three ways to count
· D-Day — D-30, D-DAY, and D+12 once it has passed
· Anniversary — how many days since you started, with every 100-day milestone
· Baby age — months and days since a birthday

■ Lunar dates and repeats
Lunar new year and harvest festival dates are converted to the solar calendar for you.
Set yearly, monthly or weekly repeat and the next date is picked automatically once one passes.

■ Cards with a picture
Pick from 26 backgrounds — travel, birthday, exam, wedding, baby and more.
Not a list of numbers, but a card that looks like the thing you are waiting for.

■ Something there from the start
New Year and Christmas are already in place. The first screen is filled before you type anything.

■ 6 languages
English · 한국어 · 日本語 · 简体中文 · Español · Français

Your records stay on your device. No account is required.
Contains ads, with an in-app purchase to remove them.
