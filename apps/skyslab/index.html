About this app

One tap. One slab. How high can you go?

Skyslab is a timing game about stopping each sliding slab at exactly the right moment. Whatever hangs over the edge is sliced off and tumbles away, so every miss makes the next slab smaller. Stay precise and the tower keeps rising.

HOW TO PLAY
• Slabs slide in from four directions. Tap anywhere on the screen to stop them.
• Any part that misses the slab below is cut off.
• Line it up exactly for a PERFECT. Each perfect in a row plays a higher note, and from the fifth one the slab grows back.
• When a slab misses the tower completely, the run ends and the camera pulls back to show your whole tower.

THE SKY CHANGES AS YOU CLIMB
Start in the meadow and rise through the cloud layer, sunset, dusk, night sky and aurora, all the way to orbit.

THINGS TO COLLECT
• A gold line marks your best height, so the next goal is always in sight.
• Beat your records to unlock 8 slab skins: Sky, Rainbow, Mint, Coral, Candy, Lava, Neon and Gold.

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
• One hand, one tap
• Sound effects, music, vibration and screen shake can each be turned off
• 12 languages, with a language picker in Settings
• Watch an ad to continue once per run, only if you want to
• The remove-ads purchase turns off banner and interstitial ads
