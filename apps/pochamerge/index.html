About this app

A cute K-food merge puzzle with adorable food friends!

Fish cake, tteokbokki, fried snacks, fish bread... merge identical foods into tastier dishes and fill your customers' orders.

■ How to play
· Tap the maker to create ingredients, then drag matching items to merge
· Fill every order within the move limit to clear the level
· Solve it in the fewest moves for 3 stars — a real challenge

■ What makes it special
· 100 stages x 20 levels — 2,000 puzzles in all
· Colorful chibi art with move-limit puzzles that keep you thinking
· A codex of 12 regular friends to collect
· Grow your own pocha through 6 stages, from a street cart to a beloved old shop
