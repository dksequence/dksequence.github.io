About this app

Whether you're buying, selling, or just curious about the phone you already own, this app puts the information you need in one place.

Mobile Status Checker combines hardware spec lookup, real-time performance monitoring, a benchmark, and used-phone inspection tools.

HARDWARE INFO
• See OS, device model, CPU, RAM, storage, battery, display, and network details
• Battery temperature, voltage, and health are shown too

REAL-TIME MONITOR
• Watch battery temperature, battery level, and RAM usage as live graphs
• A stress test shows how the device behaves under load

BENCHMARK
• Measures integer, floating-point, and memory throughput and combines them into one score
• Scores are not directly comparable to other benchmark apps — the methods differ

USED-PHONE SPEC CHECK
• Compares your device's RAM, core count, and battery design capacity against official specs for major Samsung Galaxy models
• If the model can't be identified or there's no reference data, it's honestly shown as "not comparable"

BATTERY HEALTH
• Compares actual charge capacity to the design capacity to estimate battery wear
• Shows a warning when battery level is too low for an accurate reading

SUSTAINED PERFORMANCE TEST
• Applies continuous load for a set time and measures how much performance is retained from the peak (throttling check)

DEVICE INSPECTION
• Run touch (grid-drag coverage check), screen (color/stuck-pixel), speaker, microphone, vibration, and sensor tests — one at a time or in sequence
• Capture the results as a shareable image card

GOOD TO KNOW
• Pick your language inside the app (12 languages)
• The app shows ads. The remove-ads purchase turns off banner and interstitial ads
• Microphone test recordings are temporary — deleted right after the test and never sent anywhere
