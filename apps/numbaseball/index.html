About this app

The classic deduction game Bulls & Cows (Number Baseball) is back — now as a duel against a bot.

■ Bot Duel
Set your own secret number and take turns guessing with the bot. A caster voice calls out every Strike, Ball and Out in real time.

■ Conquest Mode
Beat 20 distinct bosses one by one! The higher the stage, the smarter the bot and the longer the number — from 3 up to 6 digits.

■ Features
- Pick your character from 4 players and face 20 bosses in Conquest
- Strike / Ball / Out deduction
- Voice play-by-play + mood BGM
