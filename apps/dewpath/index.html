About this app

Slide the channel pieces into one line and send a drop of dew down to the seed.

Pieces lie scattered on a stone garden. Each one slides a single square toward the open space,
and bolted pieces do not move at all. Once the channel runs from end to end,
the dew leaves the basin and reaches the seed in the pot.

■ What makes it different
· Every board can be solved. Each one is built by laying a finished channel and then
shuffling it apart, so an unsolvable board cannot occur.
· The target move count is not an estimate. It is the shortest solution, computed for that board.
· Stuck? The hint shows you the finished shape. It does not play a move for you.
· Undo always works, and it takes the move count back with it.
· All that gets saved is the level number and your records. The same number is the same board on any device.

■ Difficulty
600 levels. Boards grow from 4×4 to 6×5, and the shortest solution runs from 6 moves early on to 42 later.

■ Controls
Just tap a piece. No dragging, no timing.

■ Three drops of dew
The dew sits on the intended route. Solve it a shorter way and you may leave some behind.

Twelve languages, switchable inside the app at any time.
