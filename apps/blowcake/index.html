About this app

BlowCake lights the candles on a birthday cake and lets you blow them out with your actual breath. Instead of tapping a button, you say "whooo" into the mic and the flames flicker, bend, and go out.

■ How it works
1. Pick a cake and light the candles.
2. Blow into the mic — the flames react to your breath and go out. (No mic? Press and hold the button instead.)
3. When every candle is out, a celebration plays and you earn points.

■ 30 photoreal cakes
Kids, elegant, love, friends, thank-you, congrats, comfort, family and seasonal — 30 cakes for every occasion. Five of them (Bear, Ivory, Love Heart, Party, Carnation) are ready from the start; unlock the rest with points you earn by blowing out candles or watching a short ad.

■ Greetings on the cake
Every cake carries a matching greeting. Tap it to switch typography styles, and each of the 12 supported languages has its own wording.

■ Features
· Realistic candle flames driven by real breath (adjustable sensitivity)
· Press-and-hold mode when a mic isn't practical
· 30 photoreal cakes, 5 ready to use
· Per-language greeting typography with style switching
· 12 languages (English, Korean, Japanese, Traditional/Simplified Chinese, Thai, Malay, Indonesian, Spanish, French, Portuguese, German)
· no account, no sign-up — start right away

■ Privacy
The microphone is only used on-device to measure how hard you are blowing. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves your phone.

Birthdays, anniversaries, a well-earned congratulations, a quiet thank-you — send it with a single candle.
