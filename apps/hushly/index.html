About this app

Pick your situation once. Your phone takes it from there.

Vibrate in class, silent in meetings, loud while driving, Do Not Disturb at night.
No more changing it by hand every time.

[Four things to choose from]
- Ring - it makes sound. You set how loud.
- Vibrate - you notice urgent calls without disturbing anyone.
- Silent - no vibration either, for places where a buzzing desk is not okay.
- Do Not Disturb - leaves your sound alone and blocks interruptions.

Add "with Do Not Disturb" on top to combine them.

[It is fine to forget]
- Decide up front how long it lasts. It releases itself when the time is up.
- While a mode is on, one quiet notification stays. Tap its "Turn off now" button and the mode ends.
- A home screen widget and a Quick Settings tile turn it off in one tap.
- Turning it off restores the sound you had. If you changed it yourself in between, we leave it.

[For the night]
Stops the video or music you left playing, after the time you set.

[Make it yours]
- Set your own name and picture. Turn "Meeting" into "Service", "Focus" into "Class".
- Days and times take one chip and two taps. There is no save button.
- Set ring, notification, media and alarm volume separately.
- Use a different ringtone for each mode.

[We also write down what we cannot do]
Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Airplane mode are closed to apps by Android - no app can change them.
Rather than pretend, we list them inside the app.

Your records stay on this device.
