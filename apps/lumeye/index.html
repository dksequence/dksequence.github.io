About this app

Lu and Mi are your two eyes.
Lu is the left one, Mi the right. How they look is computed from your real records, not made up.

■ Reminders that actually arrive
If an eye break app has ever let you down, it is usually because the reminder quietly stopped.
Lumeye runs a foreground service (plus exact alarms where your phone allows them) and restores itself after a reboot.
If something still blocks it, the "Notifications not arriving?" screen shows your real battery
optimization state, notification permission and service uptime, and sends you straight to the
right settings page for your phone maker.

■ A window after every twenty seconds
Finish a break and you receive a view through a window. 192 of them, across seasons and hours.
During the break the screen dims itself, because telling you to rest while holding your eyes
on a screen would be a contradiction.

■ Eye self-check
· Match your screen size once with a credit card, fix the distance, then begin
· Eight-direction optotypes in a staircase, so guessing rarely gets you through
· Results in 20/20, decimal and logMAR at the same time
· The color check has an "I see nothing" button on every plate
· Measurement conditions (distance, screen calibration, light, glasses) are printed with the
result, and when the conditions are poor we do not give you a number at all

■ Eye stretches guided by sound
High tone means up, low tone means down, left and right come from that side.
You do not have to watch the screen while moving your eyes. Skip and pause are always there.

■ Eye notes
Blue-light glasses, carrots and night vision, the idea that glasses make your eyes worse:
what is supported by evidence and what is not, each with its source.

■ What this app does not do
This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical
condition. Self-check results are for reference only and cannot replace an eye examination.
