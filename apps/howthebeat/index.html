About this app

Turn the knob. A formula writes the beat.

HOW THE BEAT turns math into rhythm. Pick a channel, choose one of 18 formulas, and turn the dial: every click changes the value and the pattern rewrites itself in time with the music. Stack kick, clap, hats, bass and lead, then cut a vertical video of your beat in one tap.

■ 18 rhythm formulas
Euclid, Fibonacci, Collatz, Logistic, Primes, Automaton, Thue-Morse, Digits, Beatty, Recamán, LCG, Gray code, Polyrhythm, Morse, Harmonics, Lorenz, Bounce, Kuramoto. Each one grooves differently, and the formula stays on screen so you can see how the beat is made.

■ Play it live
· 8 pads you can play with your fingers
· Record what you play — notes snap to the nearest 1/16 step
· USB MIDI keyboard support

■ Build a full song
· 16-step studio grid, piano roll and chords
· Song mode: arrange patterns bar by bar
· 108 drum kits, 40 sampled instruments, 94 synth presets
· 100 genre starter songs to begin from
· Mixer with EQ, reverb send, sidechain duck and effects
· Automation lanes for volume, pan and filter

■ Your voice and your tracks
· Mic looper with layers and echo cancel
· Beatbox sampler: hit the mic, it slices your sounds into pads
· Import WAV, MP3 or FLAC into your song
· AI split: separate vocals and instrumental on the device

■ DJ
· Two decks, cue points, loops, tempo sync and filters
· Record a mix and save it as a DJ video

■ Share
· Make a 1080p 9:16 short of your beat — one short ad per video (PRO: 60fps, ads removed)
· Export a WAV mix

■ HOW THE BEAT PRO (one-time purchase)
· 1080p 60fps shorts up to 60 seconds
· Unlimited AI split
· MIDI export
· Songs longer than 16 bars
· Ads removed

12 languages. Includes ads; PRO removes them.
