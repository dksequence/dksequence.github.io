About this app

QuitClock counts every second since you started quitting smoking, drinking, or any habit you choose, and shows how much money you have saved along the way.

No login, no community, no repeating pop-ups you cannot turn off. If a craving hits, open the SOS breathing screen — it stays completely uninterrupted, because the moment you are fighting an urge is not the moment to interrupt you.

■ Features
· Live elapsed time counter (days, hours, minutes, seconds)
· Money saved calculator for smoking, drinking, or a custom habit
· Health milestone timeline based on public health sources
· Uninterrupted craving SOS breathing screen
· Home screen widget
· Milestone notifications you can turn off for good
· All data stays on your device

※ Health milestone information is for general reference only and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
