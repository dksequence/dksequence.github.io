About this app

Your script sits right under the lens. Keep your eyes on the camera and roll.

DK Teleprompter places your script directly under your phone's front camera so your eyes stay on the lens. Paste a script, tap the red button, and recording and scrolling start together after a countdown.

■ Script band under the lens
· Reads where your camera is and places the band right below it
· Adjust band height, text size, line spacing, colors and background
· Reading marker on the left, right or both sides — arrow, underline or band

■ Takes survive interruptions
· Recording is saved in short pieces, so if the app closes because of a call or a dead battery, you lose only the last few seconds
· Interrupted takes are restored the next time you open the app
· Three modes: video, voice only, and read only for rehearsal

■ Timing that fits your language
· Reading time is counted for each writing system — Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Thai, English and more
· Set a target time (30 s, 60 s…) and the speed adjusts automatically
· Learns the speed you actually read at and uses it next time
· Shows elapsed time, time left, and how far ahead of or behind your target you are

■ Teleprompter rig (beam splitter) mode
· Mirrored text that reads correctly in the glass, with an optional vertical flip
· Portrait or landscape, side margins to fit the hood
· Maximum screen brightness while playing
· Show the script on an external display such as HDMI or wireless display

■ Remotes and phone remote
· Bluetooth remotes, foot pedals, keyboards and selfie remotes (volume button) for play, pause, speed and line jumps
· Assign any key to any action
· Use another phone on the same Wi‑Fi as a remote (4-digit code)

■ Working with scripts
· Import txt, Word (.docx) and PDF files, or receive text shared from other apps
· Split scripts into sections with “# Title” and jump between them
· Emphasis, [notes you don't read aloud] and / pauses
· Edit the script during playback and continue from the same spot

■ Takes library
· Takes grouped by script, mark your best
· Save to gallery or share, no watermark

■ Pro (one-time purchase, not a subscription)
· Keep as many scripts as you like (3 included)
· Removes the banner ad from the script library
· Voice-follow scrolling that keeps up with you

Start right away, no account needed. Available in 12 languages.
