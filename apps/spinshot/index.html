About this app

From the corner table tennis club to the world stage. One racket, one career.

■ Spin is everything
Drive, chop, smash. The direction and force of your swing set the spin, and the spin decides how the ball comes off your opponent's rubber. Same line, different spin, different ball.

■ Rubber wears out
Every rally wears the sheet down, and worn rubber grips less. The wear gauge at the bottom of the screen shows exactly where your racket stands. Re-sheet now, or squeeze one more match out of it? That is a decision too.

■ Gear actually enters the physics
Spin Master, Speed Blue, Control Green, Anti Spin. Each rubber has its own spin, speed and stability, and those numbers go straight into the ball's path. Blades too.

■ Every player has a style
Yuna takes spin well. Marcus hits heavy. Coach Jung returns balls he has no business returning. Erik splits his strength between spin and power. Who you pick changes how you play, and every dojo has its own master waiting.

■ Five difficulty tiers
Easy, Normal, Hard, Master, Veteran. The reward climbs and the gaps close.

■ Career
Corner club, school gym, community centre, office league, club championship, city trials. Every stage has its own opponent and its own goal.

■ 12 languages with an in-app language picker

A match is short. A racket lasts.
