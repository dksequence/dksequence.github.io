About this app

"Wait, I can text in Korean now!" — that's you, in just a few days.

Many people study Korean, but few can actually type it.
TADAK-KOREAN teaches Hangul typing through a messenger conversation.

■ Two rooms
1. Speed Room - see the Korean word your friend sends, and type it. Your hands remember.
2. Quiz Room - see the meaning or a picture, then recall the Korean. Your mind remembers.

■ When it scrolls away, look back up
Questions pile up faster than you can answer. Swipe up to see the ones you missed -
and the moment you start typing, the screen returns on its own.
So the real skill here is memory, not speed.

■ A 2-set keyboard the app draws itself
No system keyboard means no predictive text doing the work for you.
You watch consonants and vowels assemble in real time and learn how Hangul actually works.
It is the standard 2-set layout Koreans really use, so the skill transfers everywhere.

■ 12 languages
Instructions, hints and praise come in your language. What you type is always Korean.
한국어 · English · 日本語 · 繁體中文 · ไทย · Bahasa Melayu · Bahasa Indonesia ·
Español · Français · Português (BR) · Deutsch · 简体中文

■ 256 words
Fandom terms, cafe and food, getting around, internet speech and everyday basics -
including current slang like gapbunssa and fandom words like maknae and aegyo.

Learn a word today, write a comment with it tomorrow. That is the goal.
