MineStep

Read the numbers, take one step. A logic puzzle across the minefield

The game above is a web build of the Android app. Get it below to keep it on your device.

About this game

You do not clear the minefield. You cross it alive. Numbers are only readable under your feet. Every step reveals a little more, and stepping on an empty tile opens up a whole safe area at once.

What it is

  • No need to open the whole board — reach the exit and you are done
  • Walk around uncertain areas. No board is lost to a coin flip
  • Plant a flag on any tile you suspect
  • One wrong step ends the run right there. There is no undo
  • 60 stages across six regions: Meadow, Dusk, Night, Midnight, Snowfield, Ashlands
  • Boards grow from 9×9 all the way to 128×128
