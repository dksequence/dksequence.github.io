Stair Dash

Climb endless crumbling stairs. Tap the matching arrow and jump over the spikes.

The game above is a web build of the Android app. Get it below to keep it on your device.

About this game

Stair Dash is a fast, addictive arcade climber on never-ending crumbling stairs! Play a courier loaded with boxes and climb the collapsing staircase one step at a time. Tap the arrow that matches the next stair — same side to go straight, opposite to turn. Easy to learn, but the rising speed will keep you on the edge.

What it is

  • Each step you leave crumbles and falls away beneath you
  • Your stack of cargo wobbles precariously as you leap
  • Rain, lightning, hail and booming thunder build up over time
  • A no-timer "slow tutorial" to learn the controls
  • 6 languages · "Continue" with coins · All records stored only on your device
