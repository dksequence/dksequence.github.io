Brick World Tour

Pick a traveler and tour 10 countries — 1,000 stages of brick breaker action.

The game above is a web build of the Android app. Get it below to keep it on your device.

About this game

The satisfying feel of shooting balls to smash bricks — now with a world tour and characters. Pick a backpacking traveler and take on a brick-breaking tour across 10 countries. 100 stages per country, and the final stage is a boss battle against that country's master! Beat them and a stamp slams onto your passport. Collect 10 stamps to become World Champion.

What it is

  • Passport stamp meta — fill your passport as you clear each country
  • Landmark bricks — the Eiffel Tower, Pyramids and Great Wall appear as brick patterns
  • Country master bosses — every 100th stage is a boss with gimmicks (shield, regen, rage — 3 phases)
  • 8 travelers — each with a different passive that shapes your play style
  • The final stage is Korea — Gyeongbokgung & Namsan Tower, a showdown with the strongest master
  • No hearts, no lives — zero stress, play as long as you like
