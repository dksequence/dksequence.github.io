Russian Roulette

Pick who's 'it' with three roulette modes. A party game for any gathering.

The game above is a web build of the Android app. Get it below to keep it on your device.

About this game

Russian Roulette is a party game for one phone. It packs three roulette modes to decide who's "it" — who takes the penalty. ① Trigger — load random bullets into the cylinder, pass the phone around, and each player taps the screen to pull the trigger. An empty chamber just clicks and you're safe; a live round goes BANG and that player is "it". ② Spin — put the phone in the middle and spin. A revolver spins fast on screen, then slows to a stop, and whoever it points at is "it". ③ Pistol — put the phone in the middle and tap Spin. A pistol whirls, then slows to a stop, and whoever the barrel points at is "it".

What it is

  • Penalty picker — toggle the built-in penalties or add your own
  • 2–10 players, no account needed
  • A light, stylized party act — no real firearms or violence
