About TwoHand Cube Timer

TwoHand Cube Timer is a speedcubing timer for Android built around two hand pads, like a dedicated hardware timer. Rest both hands on the pads, wait for the pads to turn green, lift a hand to start and tap the screen to stop. This page is a small browser demo of the same flow, using the Space key.

■ In the Android app
· Scrambles for 11 events: 3x3, 2x2, 4x4, 5x5, 6x6, 7x7, Megaminx, Pyraminx, Skewb, 3x3 one-handed and 3x3 blindfolded
· Stats: best, mean, ao5, ao12 and ao100 with a progress graph
· Solve management: +2 / DNF / delete, split sessions, copy as CSV
· 1v1 duel: two people, two sets of pads, first to 3 wins
· 50 screen skins
· Cube Study for F2L, OLL and PLL
· 12 languages with an in-app language picker

The demo on this page runs in your browser only: nothing you solve is sent anywhere or saved. It uses a simplified scramble generator and a single skin.
